# Construction ERP - Step-by-Step Demo Guide

**Password for every demo account:** `Admin@12345`

For **Comment 8** features (departments, My Work, subtask roles, task detail fields), see [`docs/COMMENT8_WALKTHROUGH.md`](docs/COMMENT8_WALKTHROUGH.md).

Open the website. Follow the steps below in order. Every step tells you exactly what to click and what to do.

---

## Step 0 - Sign in

1. Open the login page.
2. Look at the **Demo accounts** panel on the right (or below on a phone).
3. Click **Project Manager**.
4. Click **Sign in**.

You are now signed in. Continue to Walkthrough 1.

---

## Walkthrough 1 - Browse projects (10 min)

**Do this as Project Manager** (click **Project Manager** in the demo panel, then click **Sign in**).

1. Click **Projects** in the left menu.
2. Look at the list. You should see project names and statuses.
3. Click the **search box**.
4. Type `LJZ`.
5. Press **Enter**.
6. Click the row **Lujiazui Mixed-Use Tower** (code **LJZ-2024-001**).
7. Look at the project dashboard on screen.
8. Click **Schedule** in the left project menu.
9. Click **Documents** in the left project menu.
10. Click **RFIs** in the left project menu.
11. Click **Projects** in the main left menu to go back to the list.

**You are done when:** You opened a project and clicked through three menu items without errors.

---

## Walkthrough 2 - Download project list to Excel (5 min)

**Do this as System Administrator** (sign out first - click your name → **Sign out** - then click **System Administrator** in the demo panel → click **Sign in**).

1. Click **Projects** in the left menu.
2. Click **Export Excel** at the top right.
3. Wait for the file to download.
4. Open the downloaded file (double-click it).
5. Scroll to the right across the columns.
6. Look for project names, types, dates, and lengths (km) or floor area (m²).

**You are done when:** The Excel file opens and shows many projects.

---

## Walkthrough 3 - Design approval (30 min)

Use project **LJZ-2024-001** for most of this. Sign out and sign in again each time the steps say to change person.

---

### 3A - Designer views design packages

1. Click your name → **Sign out**.
2. Click **Designer** in the demo panel.
3. Click **Sign in**.
4. Click **Projects**.
5. Click **LJZ-2024-001** (Lujiazui Mixed-Use Tower).
6. Click **Schedule** in the project menu.
7. Click a **task** row in the list.
8. Click **Design packages** (or open the packages tab/section).
9. Click one **design package** in the list.
10. Read the **status** on screen (e.g. In review, Approved).
11. Click an **attachment** or document name if you see one.
12. Scroll down and read who must **approve** the package.

**You are done when:** You see at least one design package with a status and documents.

---

### 3B - Reviewer opens review inbox

1. Click your name → **Sign out**.
2. Click **Reviewer** in the demo panel.
3. Click **Sign in**.
4. Click **Projects**.
5. Click **LJZ-2024-001**.
6. Click **Review inbox** in the menu (or in the top bar).
7. Click one item in the inbox list.
8. Read the review details on screen.
9. Scroll to **Comments** if you see them.
10. Click **Approve** or **Return for revision** if those buttons appear (clicking is optional - just see that they are there).

**You are done when:** You see items in the review inbox and opened one.

---

### 3C - Discipline Lead

1. Click your name → **Sign out**.
2. Click **Discipline Lead** in the demo panel.
3. Click **Sign in**.
4. Click **Projects** → click **LJZ-2024-001**.
5. Click **Review inbox**.
6. Look at the list. Open one item if any appear.

**You are done when:** You checked the Lead inbox (items may or may not be there - that is OK).

---

### 3D - Department Chief

1. Click your name → **Sign out**.
2. Click **Department Chief** in the demo panel.
3. Click **Sign in**.
4. Click **Projects** → click **DEMO-002** (Airport Expansion).
5. Click **Review inbox**.
6. Open one item if any appear.

**You are done when:** You opened the Chief inbox on DEMO-002.

---

### 3E - Institute Chief

1. Click your name → **Sign out**.
2. Click **Institute Chief** in the demo panel.
3. Click **Sign in**.
4. Click **Projects** → click **LJZ-2024-001**.
5. Click **Review inbox**.
6. Open one item if any appear.

**You are done when:** You checked the Institute Chief inbox on LJZ.

---

### 3F - Discipline Owner

1. Click your name → **Sign out**.
2. Click **Discipline Owner** in the demo panel.
3. Click **Sign in**.
4. Click **Projects** → click **DEMO-001** (Marina Tower).
5. Click **Review inbox**.
6. Open one item if any appear.
7. Click a **comment** if you see one.
8. Read whether the comment is **open** or **resolved**.

**You are done when:** You opened a review on DEMO-001 and saw comments.

---

### 3G - See a revision (optional)

1. Click your name → **Sign out**.
2. Click **Designer** in the demo panel.
3. Click **Sign in**.
4. Click **Projects** → click **DEMO-001**.
5. Click **Schedule** → open a task → click **Design packages**.
6. Click a package that was **returned** or has **Rev B** (or a second revision).
7. Compare **Rev A** and **Rev B** if both appear.

**You are done when:** You see that a document can have more than one revision.

## Walkthrough 4 - Closed project (5 min)

**Do this as Project Manager.**

1. Click your name → **Sign out**.
2. Click **Project Manager** in the demo panel.
3. Click **Sign in**.
4. Click **Projects**.
5. Click **ZMC-2023-002** (Zhongshan Medical Centre).
6. Look at the status - it should say **Closed**.
7. Click **Schedule** → open a task → click **Design packages**.
8. Click a package and check the status says **Approved**.
9. Click **Documents** in the project menu.
10. Click a **folder**, then click a file inside if listed.
11. Click **Closeout** or **Punch list** in the menu if you see it.
12. Open one record in that section.

**You are done when:** You viewed a closed project with approved packages and documents.

---

## Walkthrough 5 - Documents (10 min)

**Do this as Document Controller.**

1. Click your name → **Sign out**.
2. Click **Document Controller** in the demo panel.
3. Click **Sign in**.
4. Click **Projects**.
5. Click **DEMO-001**.
6. Click **Documents** in the project menu.
7. Click the first **folder**.
8. Click the second **folder** if there is one.
9. Click **Drawings** in the menu if you see it.
10. Click one drawing row to open it.
11. Click **Submittals** in the menu if you see it.
12. Click one submittal row to open it.

**You are done when:** You clicked through folders and opened at least one drawing or submittal.

---

## Walkthrough 6 - Site records (10 min)

**Do this as Site Engineer.**

1. Click your name → **Sign out**.
2. Click **Site Engineer** in the demo panel.
3. Click **Sign in**.
4. Click **Projects**.
5. Click **DEMO-001**.
6. Click **Daily logs** in the project menu.
7. Click one daily log row. Read it. Click **Back** or close.
8. Click **RFIs** in the project menu.
9. Click one RFI row. Read it. Click **Back** or close.

11. Click one issue row. Read it.
12. Click **Meetings** in the project menu.
13. Click one meeting row. Read it.

**You are done when:** You opened one record in each of Daily logs, RFIs, Issues, and Meetings.

## Walkthrough 7 - User list (5 min)

**Do this as System Administrator.**

1. Click your name → **Sign out**.
2. Click **System Administrator** in the demo panel.
3. Click **Sign in**.
4. Click **Settings** in the left menu (or **Administration**).
5. Click **Users**.
6. Scroll through the list of people.
7. Click one **user row** (e.g. Project Manager).
8. Read their name, email, and roles on screen.
9. Click **Back** to return to the list.

**You are done when:** You saw the user list and opened one user’s details.

---

## Walkthrough 8 - Download task list (5 min)

**Do this as Project Manager.**

1. Click your name → **Sign out**.
2. Click **Project Manager** in the demo panel.
3. Click **Sign in**.
4. Click **Projects**.
5. Click **LJZ-2024-001**.
6. On the project dashboard, click **Export task list** (or **Export** → **Task list**).
7. Wait for the file to download.
8. Open the file.
9. Scroll right across the many columns.

**You are done when:** A large Excel file downloads and opens.

---

## Walkthrough 9 - Different project types (5 min)

**Do this as Project Manager.**

1. Click **Projects** in the left menu.
2. Click **New project** at the top right.
3. Click the **Project type** dropdown.
4. Scroll the list - look for Road, Railway, Building, Airport, etc.
5. Click **Cancel** to close the form without saving.
6. In the search box, type `TYPE-ROAD`.
7. Press **Enter**.
8. Click **TYPE-ROAD-001**.
9. Read the project details - look for **road length**.
10. Click **Projects** to go back.
11. Search `TYPE-BLD`, press **Enter**, click **TYPE-BLD-001**.
12. Read the project details - look for **floor area**.

**You are done when:** You saw the type dropdown and opened a road project and a building project.

## If something looks wrong

| You see this | Do this |
|--------------|---------|
| Warning about demo email on Forgot password | Ignore it - demo accounts do not get real emails |
| A menu item is missing | Sign in as a different role from the table above |
| Review inbox is empty | Try another project (LJZ, DEMO-001, or DEMO-002) or another role |
| File will not open | Tell your facilitator |
