# Overtime (OT)

You record overtime on one sheet per month. You add an entry each time you work overtime, then submit the sheet once for approval. The sheet sorts your hours into Normal, Evening, Weekend and Holiday overtime for you.

**Find your part of this guide**

| You are | Read these sections |
| --- | --- |
| Employee | Opening your sheet, adding entries, submitting, changing, cancelling, following your sheets, and what to do if it is returned |
| Team Lead, Department Head, Discipline Lead, HR Manager, General Manager | How to approve, return or reject an overtime sheet, and how to file overtime for someone in your team |
| HR (HR Manager, HR Assistant, HR Specialist) | Everything above, plus the HR sections at the end |

---

## Rules to know first

*(Everyone who records overtime)*

- Only time **outside your normal working hours** counts. If you pick hours inside your schedule, you see *"These hours are within your normal working hours."*
- From Monday to Friday, **12:30–13:30 is lunch** and is never overtime.
- On a day with **approved leave**, the hours the leave covers cannot be claimed.
- On an **approved site assignment** day, overtime is still only outside your normal working hours (same rule as an office day), unless HR has turned on “office hours claimable on site days”.
- You **cannot open a sheet for a future month** or record a day that has not happened yet.
- Each entry must be on **one calendar day**. If you work past midnight, end the first entry at 24:00 and add a second entry on the next day starting at 00:00.
- Two entries on the same day cannot overlap.
- Once the month's payroll is finalized, the sheet shows *"This OT is locked in the current payroll run and cannot be edited"*, and nothing can be changed.

## How to open your overtime sheet

*(Employees)*

1. In the left menu, go to **New Request**.
2. Click **Overtime Application**. The **Add Overtime Sheet** page opens.
3. The page shows the **current month** only. Employees cannot open a sheet for a previous month. (HR and supervisors who file on behalf of others can still choose a past month.)
4. Click **Open sheet**.
   - If you already have a draft for this month, the page says so. Click **Continue draft**.
   - If you already submitted a sheet for this month, click **Open it**.


> **After you open it:** Your sheet opens with the status **Draft**. Add your entries next.

---

## How to add an overtime entry

*(Employees)*

1. On your sheet, click **Add entry**.
2. **Date** (required): choose a day in the sheet's month that is not in the future.
3. **Start** and **End** (required): choose the times. The lists only show times that can count as overtime that day. If none are available, you see *"No overtime times are available for this day."*
4. If you worked past midnight, click **Add next-day entry** to add the rest on the next day.
5. **Task Description** (required): describe the work you did.
6. Check the **Hours preview**. It shows **Normal OT**, **Evening OT** (22:00–06:00), **Weekend**, **Holiday** and **Total**.
7. Click **Save**.


> **After you save:** The entry appears under **Entries** and the totals update. If an entry shows **Attendance check**, the times do not match your attendance scans for that day. You can still save and submit.

## How to attach files to your sheet

*(Employees)*

1. Open your sheet.
2. Click the **plus (+)** sign on the entries row. 
2. Under **Attachments**, click **Attach files** and choose the files, for example a signed time record.
3. To remove a file, click **Remove** next to it and confirm.

---

## How to submit your sheet

*(Employees)*

1. Open your sheet. It must have at least one entry.
2. Click **Submit Sheet**.
3. In **Submit overtime sheet?**, confirm.


> **After you submit:** Your sheet has been sent for approval and its status is **Submitted**. You will receive a notification when it is approved or returned. You do not need to do anything else unless it is returned to you.

---

## How to change your sheet

*(Employees)*

1. Open your sheet (see *How to follow your overtime sheets*).
2. On the entry you want to change, click **⋯** → **Update**. To add another day, click **Add entry**.
3. Make the change and click **Save**.

- **Draft** or **Returned** sheet: your change is saved. Submit the sheet when you are ready.
- **Submitted** or **Approved** sheet: you can still change it until payroll locks the month. When you save, you see *"Your overtime request has been updated and sent for approval again"*. The sheet goes through approval again from the beginning.
- **Locked by payroll**: no changes are possible.

---

## How to cancel your sheet

*(Employees — while the status is **Draft** or **Submitted**)*

1. Open your sheet.
2. Click **Cancel Sheet**.
3. In **Cancel overtime sheet?**, confirm. The sheet and its approval request are cancelled.

---

## How to follow your overtime sheets

*(Employees)*

1. In the left menu, go to **My Profile** and click the **Overtime** tab.
2. **Overtime this year** shows your Normal, Evening, Weekend and Holiday hours. Change the **Year** to see another year.
3. Under **Overtime history**, click **View all** to see every sheet with its **Sheet #**, **Period**, hours, **Total (hrs)** and **Status**.
4. Click a sheet to open it.

**What the statuses mean**

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | You are still adding entries. |
| Submitted | Sent for approval. |
| In Review | Being approved. |
| Returned | Sent back to you to change. |
| Approved | Approved. |
| Rejected | Not approved. The sheet is closed. |
| Cancelled | Cancelled. |

A **Resubmitted** badge means the sheet was changed and sent for approval again.

---

## What to do if your sheet is returned

*(Employees)*

If your sheet is returned, you receive the notification **Approval returned**. Open the sheet to see the reason.

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** → **Pending My Action** (or **My Requests**) and open the sheet with status **Returned**. The banner says **This request was returned** and shows the approver's note.
2. Read the note.
3. Open the sheet in one of these ways:
   - On the same request page, click **Edit** at the top, or **Update** on the sheet summary.
   - Or go to **My Profile** → **Overtime**, click **View all**, and click the sheet.
4. Change the entries as asked and click **Save**.
5. Click **Submit Sheet** and confirm. The sheet goes back through the approval chain from the beginning.

> **Note:** Overtime has no **Resubmit** button in **Approvals**. Submitting the sheet again is how a returned sheet is resubmitted, because it also updates the sheet totals.

## How to approve, return or reject an overtime sheet

*(Team Lead, Department Head, Discipline Lead, HR Manager, General Manager)*

When an overtime sheet reaches you, you receive the notification **Approval required**.

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** and click the **Pending My Action** tab.
2. Click the overtime sheet. Check each entry's **Date**, **Time**, **Hours** and **Task Description**, and the totals.
3. Look for these before you decide:
   - **Attendance check** on an entry: the times do not match the employee's attendance scans.
   - A banner saying the sheet contains manually flagged holiday dates not in the public holiday calendar. Check those dates.
   - **Edited** and **This request was edited after approval**: the employee changed the sheet after an earlier approval. The table shows each change as **Previously** and **Now**.
4. Choose one:
   - **Approve**: click **Approve**, add a **Note (optional)**, and click **Approve**.
   - **Return**: click **Return**, write a **Note for submitter**, and click **Return**. The employee is notified and can change and resubmit the sheet.
   - **Reject**: click **Reject**, write the **Rejection reason**, and click **Reject**. This closes the sheet and cannot be undone.
   - **Conditionally Approve**: write the **Condition** and click **Submit**.

## How to file overtime for someone in your team

*(Department Head, Discipline Lead, HR)*

1. In the left menu, go to **New Request** and click **Overtime Application**.
2. Choose the **Month**, and the **Employee** you are filing for.
3. Click **Open sheet**, add the entries, and click **Submit Sheet**, as described above.

The sheet belongs to the employee and follows their own approvers.

## How to see all overtime sheets

*(HR)*

1. In the left menu, go to **Time & Attendance** → **Overtime**. The **Overtime Sheets** list opens.
2. The counters show **Total**, **Pending**, **Approved** and **Rejected**.
3. Search by sheet number, or filter by **Employee**, **Month** and **Year**. Click **Reset** to clear the filters.
4. The columns show **Sheet #**, **Employee**, **Period**, **Normal OT (hrs)**, **Evening OT (hrs)**, **Weekend (hrs)**, **Holiday (hrs)**, **Total (hrs)** and **Status**.
5. Click a sheet to open it.

## How to change or delete overtime

*(HR Manager)*

- **Change an entry:** open the sheet, click **⋯** → **Update** on the entry, change it, and click **Save**.
- **Delete an entry:** click **⋯** → **Delete** on the entry, then confirm **Delete entry?**. The totals are recalculated.
- **Delete a sheet:** open the sheet, click **Delete**, and confirm **Delete overtime sheet?**. The sheet is removed and its approval request is cancelled. This cannot be undone.

## How to import overtime from Excel

*(HR Manager, General Manager, Managing Director)*

1. Go to **Time & Attendance** → **Overtime** and click **Import from Excel** to import for many employees. To import into one sheet only, open that sheet and click **Import from Excel** there.
2. **Upload**: click **Download template** and keep its header row. Fill one row per entry: **employeeEmail** or **employeeId**, **date**, **startTime**, **endTime**, **taskDescription**, and **isHoliday** if the day is a holiday.
3. Drop the .xlsx file into the box. It can be up to 5 MB and 500 rows.
4. **Review**: check the rows marked **Ready** and **Invalid**. Fix the invalid rows and upload again if needed.
5. Click **Import N rows**.

> **Note:** Entries can only be imported into sheets that are **Draft** or **Returned**.

## Things HR should know

*(HR)*

- **Payroll lock.** After the month's payroll is finalized, overtime for that month cannot be changed by anyone.
- **Manually flagged holidays.** If an imported or entered day is marked as a holiday but is not in the public holiday calendar, approvers see a warning. Add the holiday under **Time & Attendance** → **Public Holidays** if it is real.
- **Changes after approval.** If an employee changes an approved sheet, it goes through approval again. Approvers see exactly what changed.
