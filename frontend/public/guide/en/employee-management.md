# Employee Management

HR uses the employee list to create and maintain employee accounts, and the contracts list to record each employee's employment contract. Employees do not manage other employees.

**Who does what**

| Role | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Employee (everyone) | Nothing here. You see your own details in **My Profile**. |
| HR and System Administrator | Create and edit employees, and manage their contracts. |

**Where to find it**
- Employees: click **People** in the left menu, then click **Employees** (users management only).
- Contracts: click **People** in the left menu, then click **Employee Contracts** (HR only).

---

## How to add an employee

1. Click **People** in the left menu, then click **Employees** and click the button to add a new employee.
2. Enter the employee's details, such as their full name, email, department and position.
3. Choose whether to set a **temporary password**. If you do, the employee must change it at first sign-in.
4. Save.

---

## How to record an employee contract

1. Click **People** in the left menu, then click **Employee Contracts**.
2. Open the employee or click to add a contract, and enter the contract dates and terms.
3. Save. The contract appears under the employee's **Contract** tab on their profile.

---

## Tips

- Keep the department and position up to date. Requests are sent to approvers based on each person's department and manager.
- Click **People** in the left menu, then **Org Gaps**, to find employees who have no supervisor or department.
