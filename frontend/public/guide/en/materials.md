# Materials & Purchases

There are three requests for getting things you need for work:

- **Purchase Request**: ask the company to buy something.
- **Material Requisition**: take materials out of the warehouse.
- **Low-Value Consumables Requisition**: ask for small, low-value items such as office or site supplies, each under ETB 5,000.

**Find your part of this guide**

| You are | Read these sections |
| --- | --- |
| Employee | Submitting each request, following it, and what to do if it is returned |
| Anyone who approves these requests | How to approve a materials or purchase request |
| Procurement, store and HR staff | Seeing all requests, correcting items, exporting and importing |

## How to submit a purchase request

*(Employees)*

1. In the left menu, go to **New Request**.
2. Click **Purchase Request**. The form has three steps: **Request Details**, **Items** and **Review & Submit**.


## Step 1 — Request Details (purchase)

*(Employees)*

1. **Title** (required): a short name for the request.
2. **Required By Date**: when you need the items.
3. **Vendor Name**, **Vendor Email** and **Vendor Phone Number**: if you already know a supplier. The email must be valid.
4. **Justification** (required): why the purchase is needed.
5. Click **Next**.

## Step 2 — Items (purchase)

*(Employees)*

1. Click **Add Item**. The **New Item** window opens.
2. Enter:
   - **Item Name** (required)
   - **Description (Model / Specifications)**, for example *"Office laptop (HP i7, 16GB RAM)"*
   - **Quantity** (required) and **Unit** (required), for example pcs, box or kg
   - **Unit Price** (required), in ETB
   - **Item Image** (optional): a JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF photo
3. Click **Save**. The line **Total** and the **Estimated Total** are worked out for you.
4. Add every item you need, and click **Next**.


## Step 3 — Review & Submit (purchase)

*(Employees)*

1. Check the details, items and **Estimated Total**.
2. Under **Supporting Documents**, click **Choose supporting documents** to attach quotes or specifications (optional).
3. Click **Back** to change anything.
4. Click **Submit Request**.

> **After you submit:** You see *"Purchase request submitted for approval"*. Your request has been sent for approval. You will receive a notification when it is approved or returned.

## How to submit a material requisition

*(Employees)*

1. In the left menu, go to **New Request**.
2. Click **Material Requisition**. The form has three steps: **Request Details**, **Items** and **Review & Submit**.


## Step 1 — Request Details (material requisition)

*(Employees)*

1. **Title** (required).
2. **Purpose** (required): what the materials are for.
3. **Required By Date** (optional).
4. **Warehouse Location** (optional): which warehouse to take them from.
5. Click **Next**.

## Step 2 — Items (material requisition)

*(Employees)*

1. Click **Add Item**. For each line, enter:
   - **Item Name** (required)
   - **Item Code**: the stock or catalogue code, if you know it
   - **Quantity** (required) and **Unit** (required)
   - **Description**
2. Click **Save**. Add at least one item.
3. Click **Next**.

## Step 3 — Review & Submit (material requisition)

*(Employees)*

1. Check the summary.
2. Under **Supporting Documents**, attach drawings or specifications if needed.
3. Click **Submit Request**.

> **After you submit:** You see *"Material requisition submitted for approval"*. Your request has been sent for approval. You will receive a notification when it is approved or returned.

## How to submit a consumables requisition

*(Employees)*

This form is for low-value consumable items only. Each item must be valued below ETB 5,000, and one request can hold up to 20 items. For more expensive items, use **Material Requisition** or **Purchase Request**.

1. In the left menu, go to **New Request**.
2. Click **Low-Value Consumables Requisition**. The form has three steps: **Request Details**, **Items** and **Review & Submit**.

## Step 1 — Request Details (consumables)

*(Employees)*

1. **Title** (required) and **Purpose** (required).
2. **Required By Date** (optional).
3. Click **Next**.

## Step 2 — Items (consumables)

*(Employees)*

1. Click **Add Item**. For each line, enter the **Item Name** (required), **Item Code**, **Model / Specification**, **Description**, **Quantity** (required) and **Unit** (required).
2. Click **Save**. Use **Update** or **Delete** on a line to change or remove it.
3. Click **Next**.

## Step 3 — Review & Submit (consumables)

*(Employees)*

1. Check the request and items.
2. Under **Declaration**, tick *"I confirm that all items in this requisition are low-value consumables with an estimated unit value below ETB 5,000 each."* You cannot submit without it.
3. Under **Supporting Documents**, attach quotes or packing lists if needed.
4. Click **Submit Request**.


> **After you submit:** You see *"Consumables requisition submitted for approval"*. Your request has been sent for approval. You will receive a notification when it is approved or returned.

### Why the form might not let you submit

- *"Add at least one line item"*.
- *"Maximum 20 items per requisition"*: split the items into two requests.
- *"Confirm the low-value consumables declaration"*: tick the declaration.

## How to follow your request

*(Employees)*

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** → **My Requests** and open the request.
2. You see the details, the items, the attachments and the status.

---

## What to do if your request is returned

*(Employees)*

If your request is returned, you receive the notification **Approval returned**. Open it to see the reason.

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** → **My Requests** and open the request with status **Returned**. The banner says **This request was returned** and shows the approver's note.
2. Read the note.
3. To change the request, click **Edit** at the top of the request (or **Edit** next to it in **My Requests**). The form opens with your details filled in, and the approver's note is shown at the top. Make your changes and click **Save**. You see *"Changes saved."* and go back to the request, which is still **Returned**. On a material requisition you can change the details and add, change or remove item lines.
4. When you are ready, click **Resubmit** at the top of the request (if nothing needs to change, you can skip editing). In **Resubmit request**, write a **Note to approver (optional)**, click **Attach files** if needed, and click **Resubmit**.

After you resubmit, the request goes back through the approval chain from the beginning.

> **After you resubmit:** You see *"Request resubmitted"*.

> **Note:** **Edit** is available for **Material Requisition** only. For a purchase or consumables request, you cannot change the item lines yourself: click **Resubmit** and say what must change in your note, or cancel the request and submit a new one. To send a new quote or document, attach it when you click **Resubmit**.

## How to approve a materials or purchase request

*(Anyone who approves these requests)*

When a request reaches you, you receive the notification **Approval required**.

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** → **Pending My Action** and open the request.
2. Check the **Request Details** and every line under **Items**. For a purchase request, check the **Unit Price**, **Total** and **Estimated Total**. Open the **Attachments**.
3. Choose **Approve**, **Return** (write a **Note for submitter**), or **Reject** (write the **Rejection reason**). The submitter is notified.

## How to see all requests

*(Procurement, store and HR staff)*

1. In the left menu, go to **Operations** → **Procurement**, and click **Purchase Requests**, **Material Requisition** or **Consumables**.
2. The counters show **Total**, **Draft**, **Pending**, **Approved** and **Rejected**.
3. Search, or filter by **Employee**. Click a request to open it.
4. To download, click **Export requests** or **Export requisitions** on the list. On an open request, click **Export items** or **Export PDF**.

## How to correct items on a draft or returned request

*(Procurement, store and HR staff)*

1. Open the request from the list. A returned request shows *"This request was returned for revision."*
2. On an item, click **Update** to change it and **Save**, or **Delete** → **Remove** to take it out. For a purchase request, the total is worked out again.
3. Click **Add Item** to add a line. On a material requisition, you can also click **Import from Excel** to add many lines at once.
4. The employee then resubmits from **Approvals**.

## Things HR should know

*(Procurement, store and HR staff)*

- **Who approves.**
  - Purchase requests always go to the General Manager.
  - Material requisitions go to the employee's manager, then the HR Officer, then the General Manager. Steps the submitter already covers by their own role are skipped.
  - Consumables requisitions go to the HR Officer only. When HR Officers or more senior staff submit their own consumables requisition, it is approved directly.
- **Cancelling.** Only the person who made a request can cancel it, while it is **Draft** or **Submitted**: open it, click **Cancel Request**, and confirm. Any open approval is cancelled too.
- **Approved directly.** A request with *"Approved directly - no approval required"* had no approvers.
- **Importing purchase requests.** **Import from Excel** on **Purchase Requests** adds item lines to **Draft** requests. Item images are not imported, and nothing is submitted automatically.
