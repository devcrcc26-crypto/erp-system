# Work From Home

Use a work-from-home request to ask to work from home on one or more days. When it is approved, your attendance for those days shows **Work from home** instead of absent.

**Find your part of this guide**

| You are | Read these sections |
| --- | --- |
| Employee | Requesting work from home, following and cancelling your request, and what to do if it is returned |
| Reports to (your supervisor), HR Officer | How to approve a work-from-home request |
| HR (HR Manager, HR Assistant, HR Specialist) | Assigning work-from-home days directly, and cancelling approved requests |

---

## How to request work from home

*(Employees)*

1. In the left menu, go to **New Request**.
2. Click **Work From Home**. The **Request work from home** form has two steps: **Details** and **Review**.


## Step 1 — Details

*(Employees)*

1. **Dates** (required): pick the first and last day. You can request up to the number of days shown on the form. Sundays, public holidays and days already covered are skipped automatically.
2. Check the list of days. Skipped days show why, for example **Sunday**, **Public holiday**, **Leave**, **Site assignment** or **Already work from home**.
3. **Reason** (required): explain why you need to work from home. Your approver sees this reason.
4. Click **Next**.

## Step 2 — Review

*(Employees)*

1. Check the dates and reason.
2. Click **Back** to change anything.
3. Click **Submit request**.

> **After you submit:** You see *"Work-from-home request submitted"*. Your request has been sent for approval. You will receive a notification when it is approved or returned.

### Why the form might not let you submit

- *"Select the start and end dates"*: no dates chosen.
- *"None of the selected days can be work from home"*: every day is a Sunday, holiday, leave day, site day or already covered.

---

## How to follow or cancel your request

*(Employees)*

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** → **My Requests** and open the request.
2. **Days in this request** lists each day as **Work from home** or **Skipped**.
3. To cancel while it is still waiting for approval, click **Cancel request**, add a **Reason (optional)**, and confirm. Nothing has been created yet, so no days are removed.

> **Note:** An approved request can only be cancelled by HR.

---

## What to do if your request is returned

*(Employees)*

If your request is returned, you receive the notification **Approval returned**. Open it to see the reason.

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** → **My Requests** and open the request with status **Returned**. The banner says **This request was returned** and shows the approver's note.
2. Read the note.
3. To change the request, click **Edit** at the top of the request (or **Edit** next to it in **My Requests**). The form opens with your details filled in, and the approver's note is shown at the top. Make your changes and click **Save**. You see *"Changes saved."* and go back to the request, which is still **Returned**. You can change the dates and the reason. The days are checked again the same way as a new request.
4. When you are ready, click **Resubmit** at the top of the request (if nothing needs to change, you can skip editing). In **Resubmit request**, write a **Note to approver (optional)**, click **Attach files** if needed, and click **Resubmit**.

After you resubmit, the request goes back through the approval chain from the beginning.

> **After you resubmit:** You see *"Request resubmitted"*.

## How to approve a work-from-home request

*(The employee's Reports to, then the HR Officer)*

A work-from-home request goes first to the employee's **Reports to** (the supervisor set on their profile), then to the **HR Officer**. When it reaches you, you receive the notification **Approval required**.

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** → **Pending My Action** and open the request.
2. Check the **Days in this request** and the **Reason**.
3. Choose **Approve**, **Return** (write a **Note for submitter**), or **Reject** (write the **Rejection reason**). The employee is notified.

## How to assign work-from-home days directly

*(HR)*

Use this for office closures or planned remote days. No approval request is sent.

1. In the left menu, go to **Time & Attendance** → **Work From Home**. **Work From Home Days** opens.
2. Click **Add**. The **Assign work-from-home days** window opens.
3. **Periods** (required): add one or more date ranges with **Add period**. Remove one with **Remove period**. Periods must not overlap.
4. **Reason**: shown on the calendar, for example *"Office closed for maintenance"*.
5. **Who works from home** (required):
   - **Everyone in the company**
   - **Specific departments**: choose the **Departments**.
   - **Specific employees**: choose the **Employees**.
6. Click **Save**.


> **After you save:** Attendance for the people covered shows **Work from home** for those days.

## How to change or remove an assigned day

*(HR)*

1. On **Work From Home Days**, choose the **Month** and **Year**. The counters show **All days**, **Company-wide**, **By department** and **Named people**.
2. On a day, click **Update** to change it, or **Delete** to remove it and confirm **Delete work-from-home day**. Attendance for that date may change.

## How to cancel an approved request

*(HR)*

1. Open the request in **Approvals**.
2. Click **Cancel request**, add a **Reason (optional)**, and confirm.

> **After you confirm:** You see *"Request cancelled - N work-from-home day(s) removed."* Attendance for those days is recalculated.
