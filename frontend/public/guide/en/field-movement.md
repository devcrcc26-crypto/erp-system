# Field Movement & Early Departure

When your monthly attendance shows a day with a problem, you explain it with a **Field Movement / Early Departure Log**. A day can be flagged for a **Missing clock-in**, a **Missing clock-out**, an **Early departure** or **No scans**. You can explain several days with the same problem in one go.

**Find your part of this guide**

| You are | Read these sections |
| --- | --- |
| Employee | Explaining flagged days, and what to do if your explanation is returned |
| Anyone who approves explanations | How to approve an explanation |
| HR (HR Manager, HR Assistant, HR Specialist) | Seeing all explanations and entering a paper form for someone |

---

## How to explain flagged days

*(Employees)*

You can explain days once HR has processed that month's attendance. When HR approves a month, you get a notification if you have days to explain.

1. In the left menu, go to **New Request**.
2. Click **Field Movement / Early Departure Log**. The form has three steps: **Choose month**, **Explain days** and **Review & submit**.


> **Note:** You can also start from **My Profile** → **Attendance**. When a month has flagged days, a banner there has a **Submit Explanation** button.

## Step 1 — Choose month

*(Employees)*

1. Choose the **Month** and **Year**.
2. Click **Check for flagged days**.
3. Read the result:
   - *"You have N days to explain for …"*: click **Next**.
   - *"No flagged days found for …"*: your attendance is complete. No explanation is needed.
   - *"Attendance for … has not been processed yet."*: wait until HR has uploaded and approved the month.
   - *"All flagged days for … already have an explanation in progress."*: you already sent an explanation for these days. Click **View in My Requests** to follow it.

## Step 2 — Explain days

*(Employees)*

The table lists each flagged day with its **Date**, **Scans (in / out)**, **Issue** and **Explanation** status.

1. Tick the days that have the same problem. Use **Select all compatible days** to tick them all. Days with a different problem cannot be explained together.
2. Click **Explain selected**, or click **Explain** on a single day.
3. Under **What happened on this day?**, choose one:
   - **Field / site visit**: you were out on work. Enter the **Time out** (required), the **Return time** (optional) and the **Location / destination** (required).
   - **Left early**: enter the **Time out** (required).
   - **Forgot to clock in or out**: under **Which scan did you forget?**, choose **Clock-in only**, **Clock-out only** or **Both clock-in and clock-out**. The **Stated clock-in time** and **Stated clock-out time** start at your scheduled times. Change them if needed.
4. **Reason / note** (required): explain what happened.
5. **Attachment** (optional): for example a site visit form.
6. To set different times for different days, open **Adjust times per day**.
7. If you are explaining a month that has already ended, you may also be asked for a **Late entry reason**.
8. Click **Save draft**. The days show as explained.
9. Repeat for any other days, then click **Next**. You do not have to explain every day at once.


> **Note:** A forgotten scan is filled in once your explanation is approved. An early departure is only excused by an approved **Left early** or **Field / site visit** explanation.

## Step 3 — Review & submit

*(Employees)*

1. Check **Review your explanation**: the **Days included** and the **Entries to create**.
2. Under *"Anything else your supervisor should know?"*, add a note if needed.
3. Click **Submit for Approval**.

> **After you submit:** You see *"Submitted for approval."* Your request has been sent for approval. You will receive a notification when it is approved or returned.

### Why you might not be able to continue

- *"Select at least one day to explain."*
- *"Choose a reason before saving."*
- *"Choose In, Out, or Both before saving."*
- *"Save at least one day as draft before submitting."*
- *"An unsubmitted draft is locking …"*: you saved a draft earlier but never sent it. Open it from the message and cancel it to unlock the days, then start again.

## What to do if your explanation is returned

*(Employees)*

If your explanation is returned, you receive the notification **Approval returned**. Open it to see the reason.

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** → **My Requests** and open the explanation with status **Returned**. The banner says **This request was returned** and shows the approver's note.
2. Read the note.
3. Click **Resubmit** at the top of the request. In **Resubmit request**, write a **Note to approver (optional)** with the missing details, and click **Attach files** to add proof if needed.
4. Click **Resubmit**. The explanation goes back through the approval chain from the beginning.

> **Note:** While an explanation is waiting for approval, you cannot pick those days again. If it is rejected or cancelled, the days can be explained again.

## How to approve an explanation

*(Anyone who approves explanations)*

When an explanation reaches you, you receive the notification **Approval required**.

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** → **Pending My Action** and open the explanation.
2. Check each day: the problem, the reason chosen, the times, the location and any attachment.
3. Choose **Approve**, **Return** (write a **Note for submitter**), or **Reject** (write the **Rejection reason**). The employee is notified.

> **After you approve:** The explanation is applied to the employee's attendance for those days.

## How to see all explanations

*(HR)*

1. In the left menu, go to **Time & Attendance** → **Field Movement Logs**.
2. Choose the **Month**, **Year**, **Status** and **Employee**.
3. The counters show **All submissions**, **Pending approval**, **Approved** (applied to attendance) and **Rejected or cancelled**.
4. The columns show **Log #**, **Employee**, **Period**, **Days**, **Status**, **Waiting on** and **Submitted**.
5. Click a log to see the **Explained days** and the **Employee note**, or click **Open in Approvals**.

## How to enter a paper form for someone

*(HR)*

1. On **Field Movement Logs**, click **Add on behalf**.
2. In **Choose month**, search for the **Employee** by name or employee ID, then choose the **Month** and **Year**. Past months are fine.
3. Click **Check for flagged days**, and continue with **Explain days** and **Review & submit** as above.

> **After you submit:** The log shows *"Submitted on behalf by [your name]"* and goes through the employee's approval chain.

## Things HR should know

*(HR)*

- **When employees can explain.** Employees can only explain a month after its attendance has been imported and approved. Approving a month notifies every employee with flagged days.
- **Locked days.** A saved but unsubmitted draft, or an explanation waiting for approval, locks its days until it is cancelled or rejected.
