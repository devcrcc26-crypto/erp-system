# Expense Claims

Use an expense claim to get back money you spent on work, such as transport, meals or accommodation. You list each expense with its receipt and send the claim for approval. Once it is approved, HR pays you back.

**Find your part of this guide**

| You are | Read these sections |
| --- | --- |
| Employee | Making a claim, following your claims, changing or cancelling a draft, and what to do if a claim is returned |
| Anyone who approves expense claims | How to approve an expense claim |
| HR (HR Manager, HR Assistant, HR Specialist) | Seeing all claims, marking a claim as paid, deleting, importing and exporting |

---

## How to make an expense claim

*(Employees)*

1. In the left menu, go to **New Request**.
2. Click **Expense Claim**. The form has three steps: **Request Details**, **Items** and **Review & Submit**.


## Step 1 — Request Details

*(Employees)*

1. **Title** (required): a short title the approver will recognize, for example *"Site visit Adama, March"*.
2. **Description** (optional): the purpose or project.
3. Click **Next**.

## Step 2 — Items

*(Employees)*

1. Click **Add Item**. The **Add Expense Item** window opens.
2. Fill in:
   - **Category** (required): for example Transport, Meals or Accommodation.
   - **Description** (required): what the expense was.
   - **Expense Date** (required): it cannot be in the future.
   - **Amount** (required): in ETB.
   - **Receipt** (required): one or more PDF, JPG or PNG files. All files you add stay on this one item (for example an invoice and its payment slip).
3. Click **Save**. The item appears in the list with its **Date**, **Category**, **Description**, **Amount** and **Receipt**.
4. Repeat for each expense. To change or remove a line, use **Update** or **Delete** on it.
5. Click **Next**.


## Step 3 — Review & Submit

*(Employees)*

1. Check the items and the **Total**.
2. Click **Back** to change anything.
3. Click **Submit Claim** to send it for approval, or click **Save as draft** to finish it later. A draft can be saved without receipts, but every item needs one before you submit.

> **After you submit:** You see *"Expense claim submitted for approval"*. Your claim has been sent for approval. You will receive a notification when it is approved or returned.

> **After you save a draft:** You see *"Draft saved. You can finish and submit it later from My Expense Claims or My Drafts."* and the claim opens with status **Draft**. See *How to finish a draft claim*.

### Why the form might not let you submit

- *"Enter a title for this claim"*: the title is empty.
- *"Add at least one expense item with a receipt"*: the claim has no items.
- *"Attach a receipt for this item"*: an item has no receipt.
- The expense date is in the future.

## How to follow your expense claims

*(Employees)*

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** and click the **My Requests** tab. Your submitted claims are listed with the approver each one is waiting for.
2. Click a claim to open it. You see each item with its receipts, the **Approval Chain**, and under **Paid** the date HR paid you. An approved claim not yet paid shows **Unpaid**.
3. Claims you saved but did not send are in **Approvals** → **My Drafts**.

If people report to you, you also have **Pay & Travel** → **Expense Claims** in the left menu. It lists your own claims and your team's, with **Total**, **Drafts**, **In Review**, **Approved** and **Rejected** counters.

**What the statuses mean**

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | Saved but not sent. |
| Submitted | Sent for approval. |
| In Review | Being approved. |
| Returned | Sent back to you to change. |
| Approved | Approved. Waiting to be paid. When paid, it shows **Paid** with the date. |
| Rejected | Not approved. |
| Cancelled | Cancelled. |

## How to finish a draft claim

*(Employees — only while the claim is **Draft**)*

A claim is a **Draft** when you clicked **Save as draft** instead of **Submit Claim**.

1. Open the draft in one of these ways:
   - **Approvals** → **My Drafts**, then click the claim (or **Edit** next to it).
   - If people report to you: **Pay & Travel** → **Expense Claims**, then click the claim with status **Draft**.
2. To change the title or description, click **Update**, make the changes, and click **Save**.
3. To add an item, click **Add Item**. To change or remove one, click **⋯** on the item and choose **Update** or **Delete**. An item can have more than one receipt file. Uploading new files on **Update** replaces its receipts.
4. If the page says *"N item(s) still need a receipt before you can submit."*, add the missing receipts.
5. Click **Submit Claim** and confirm **Submit expense claim?**.

---

## How to cancel an expense claim

*(Employees — while the claim is **Draft** or **Submitted**)*

1. Open the claim.
2. Click **Cancel claim**.
3. Confirm **Cancel expense claim?**. The claim and its approval request are cancelled.

---

## What to do if your claim is returned

*(Employees)*

If your claim is returned, you receive the notification **Approval returned**. Open the claim to see the reason.

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** → **My Requests** and open the claim with status **Returned**. The banner says **This request was returned** and shows the approver's note.
2. Read the note.
3. To change the claim, click **Edit** at the top of the claim (or **Edit** next to it in **My Requests**). The edit page shows the approver's note at the top.
4. Change the **Title** or **Description** if needed. Under **Items**, click **Add Item** to add one, or use the edit and delete buttons on an item. Item changes are saved straight away, and every item needs a receipt.
5. Click **Save**. You see *"Changes saved."* and go back to the request, which is still **Returned**.
6. When you are ready, click **Resubmit** at the top of the request (if nothing needs to change, you can skip editing). In **Resubmit request**, write a **Note to approver (optional)**, click **Attach files** if needed, and click **Resubmit**.

After you resubmit, the claim goes back through the approval chain from the beginning.

## How to approve an expense claim

*(Anyone who approves expense claims)*

When a claim reaches you, you receive the notification **Approval required**.

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** → **Pending My Action** and open the claim.
2. Check each item's **Date**, **Category**, **Description** and **Amount**. Click **Preview** to see a receipt.
3. Choose **Approve**, **Return** (write a **Note for submitter**), or **Reject** (write the **Rejection reason**). The employee is notified.

See the **Approvals Inbox** topic for the full steps.

## How to see all expense claims

*(HR)*

1. In the left menu, go to **Pay & Travel** → **Expense Claims**. **Expense Claims** opens.
2. Search by claim number, title or employee, or filter by **Employee**. Click **Reset** to clear the filters.
3. The list adds the **Employee** column.
4. Click a claim to open it.

## How to mark a claim as paid

*(HR — approved claims only)*

1. Open the approved claim.
2. Click **Mark as paid**. The **Mark expense claim as paid?** window opens.
3. **Payment reference** (optional): the PV or bank reference.
4. Confirm.

> **After you confirm:** You see *"Expense claim marked as paid."* The claim shows **Paid**, **Paid on** and **Recorded by**. The employee sees that it was paid.

## How to delete an expense claim

*(HR)*

1. Open the claim and click **Delete**.
2. Confirm **Delete expense claim?**. The claim is removed and its approval request is cancelled. This cannot be undone.

## How to import or export expense claims

*(HR)*

- **Export:** on **Expense Claims**, click **Export Excel**. The rows matching your search and filters are exported.
- **Import claims:** click **Import from Excel**, then **Download template**. Each row needs **employeeEmail** or **employeeId** and a **title**, and adds one item to a **Draft** claim. Rows with the same employee and title go into the same claim. Receipts are not imported, and claims are not submitted automatically: the receipts must be uploaded before the claim is submitted.
- **Import items into a draft:** open a **Draft** claim and click **Import from Excel** to add many items at once.
