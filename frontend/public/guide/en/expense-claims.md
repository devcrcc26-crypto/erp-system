# Expense Claims

Use an expense claim to get back money you spent on work: transport, meals, accommodation and similar. You list each expense with a receipt, review the claim, and submit it for approval.

**Who does what**

| Role | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Employee (everyone) | Submit your own expense claims and track them in **Approvals** and by clicking **Pay & Travel** in the left menu, then **Expense Claims**. |
| Approvers | Approve the claim when it reaches you in **Approvals**. |
| HR (HR Manager, HR Assistant, HR Specialist) | See everyone's expense claims by clicking **Pay & Travel** in the left menu, then **Expense Claims** and process them. |

**Where to find it**
- Submit: click **New Request** in the left menu, then click **Expense Claim**.
- Track: click **Pay & Travel** in the left menu, then click **Expense Claims**. You see your own claims; HR sees everyone's.

---

## How to submit an expense claim

1. Click **New Request** in the left menu, then click **Expense Claim**.
2. **Request Details:** enter a **Title** that approvers will recognize, and an optional **Description** (purpose, project, and so on).
3. **Items:** click **Add Item** for each expense. For each item, enter:
   - **Expense Date**
   - **Category** (for example Transport, Meals, Accommodation)
   - **Description**
   - **Amount**
   - **Receipt**: a PDF or image. Every item needs a receipt.
   - Click **Save** to add the item to the list. Use **Edit Item** or **Delete** to change or remove a line.
   - Amounts are converted to ETB automatically for the approval total.
4. **Review & Submit:** check the items and the **Total**, then click **Submit Claim**.

> **What happens next:** The claim enters the approval chain and you can follow it in **Approvals**. If an approver returns it, edit it there and resubmit.

---

## Things that stop a submission

- No title.
- No items. At least one item with a receipt is required.
- An item without a category, description, date, amount or receipt.

---

## Tips

- Attach the receipt when you add the item, not later. A claim with a missing receipt cannot be submitted.
- Keep one claim per trip or purpose so approvers can check each one quickly.
