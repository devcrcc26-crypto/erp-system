# Evaluations

Performance evaluations run every quarter. In each evaluation, you assess yourself first, then your Team Lead assesses you, then your Discipline Lead. The Discipline Lead shares the result with you, and you accept it or dispute it.

**Find your part of this guide**

| You are | Read these sections |
| --- | --- |
| Employee | Doing your self assessment, reading your result, and disputing it |
| Team Lead | Assessing your team members |
| Discipline Lead | Assessing, sharing results, and responding to disputes |
| HR (HR Manager, HR Assistant, HR Specialist) | Templates, cycles, the evaluation list, escalated disputes and filing |

**How an evaluation moves**

The status shows where an evaluation is: **Pending** → **Self-Assessed** → **Team Lead Assessed** → **DL Assessed** → **Result Shared** → **Accepted** → **Locked**. If the result is disputed, it can also show **Disputed**, **DL Upheld**, **DL Adjusted**, **Escalated to HR** or **HR Decided**.

---

## How to do your self assessment

*(Employees)*

1. In the left menu, go to **My Profile** and click the **Evaluations** tab. **My Evaluations** lists your quarterly evaluations, with your **Performance Trend**.
2. An evaluation that needs you shows **Action Required**. Open it.
3. Under **Self Assessment** (marked **Your turn**), rate each criterion from **1** to **5**, or choose **N/A** if it does not apply. Each section shows its weight.
4. Add a **Comment (optional)** to any criterion.
5. Click **Submit Assessment**.


> **After you submit:** You see *"Assessment submitted successfully"*. Your evaluation moves on to the next assessment. Until your Team Lead starts, you can open it again and click **Re-assess** to change your answers.

---

## How to read your result

*(Employees)*

1. When your result is shared, open the evaluation from **My Profile** → **Evaluations**.
2. Under **Result & Dispute**, read the **Final Result** and **Final Score**, and the assessments.
3. Choose one:
   - Click **Accept** if you agree. You see *"Result accepted"*.
   - Click **Dispute** if you do not agree. See the next section.

> **Important:** You have 5 days to respond. If you do nothing, the result is accepted automatically.

---

## What to do if you disagree with your result

*(Employees)*

1. Click **Dispute**.
2. Write the **Reason for dispute (required)**. Explain which scores you disagree with and why.
3. Submit. You see *"Dispute submitted"*. Your Discipline Lead has 5 days to respond.
4. When the response comes back, open the evaluation again. The **Decision** shows whether the score was upheld or adjusted.
5. Choose one:
   - Click **Accept decision** to close the dispute.
   - Click **Escalate to HR**, write the **Reason for escalation to HR (required)**, and submit. HR then makes the final decision.

> **Note:** If the Discipline Lead adjusts the score and you do not respond within 5 days, the adjusted result is accepted automatically. All steps are listed under **Dispute History**.

## How to assess a team member

*(Team Leads)*

1. In the left menu, go to **My Evaluation Inbox**. The **Needs My Action** tab lists the evaluations waiting for you.
2. Open the evaluation. You can see the employee's **Self Assessment**.
3. Under **Team Lead Assessment**, rate each criterion **1**–**5** or **N/A**, and add comments.
4. Write your overall comments about the employee's performance.
5. Click **Submit Assessment**.


> **After you submit:** The evaluation moves to the Discipline Lead. Until they start, you can click **Re-assess** to change your scores.

The other tabs in the inbox are **In Progress**, **Awaiting Employee**, **Escalated to HR** and **Completed**. Search by name or employee ID.

## How to assess and share a result

*(Discipline Leads)*

1. In the left menu, go to **My Evaluation Inbox** and open an evaluation from **Needs My Action**.
2. Read the **Self Assessment** and the **Team Lead Assessment**.
3. Under **Discipline Lead Assessment**, rate each criterion and add comments, then click **Submit Assessment**. The status becomes **DL Assessed**.
4. Click **Share Result**.

> **After you share:** You see *"Result shared with employee"*. The employee has 5 days to accept or dispute.

## How to respond to a dispute

*(Discipline Leads)*

1. Open the evaluation with status **Disputed**. Read the **Employee's dispute reason**.
2. Under **Respond to Dispute**, choose one:
   - **Uphold original score**: write why the original score stands and submit. You see *"Score upheld"*.
   - **Adjust score (re-open assessment form)**: change the criterion scores, then click **Resubmit Adjusted Assessment**. The new final score replaces the old one.
3. The employee then accepts your decision or escalates it to HR.

> **Important:** You have 5 days to respond. If you do not, the original score is upheld automatically.

## How to set up an evaluation template

*(HR)*

1. In the left menu, go to **People** → **Evaluation Templates**.
2. Click **New Template**.
3. Enter the **Template Name** (required) and a **Description**.
4. Under **Sections**, add each section with its name in English and Chinese, its weight, and its criteria. Each criterion has an English name, a Chinese name and an optional description. Use **Remove section** or **Remove criterion** to delete one.
5. Save the template.

> **Note:** To change a template that is already in use, click **New Version** on it. Evaluations that were already created keep the old version.

## How to run an evaluation cycle

*(HR)*

1. In the left menu, go to **People** → **Evaluation Cycles**.
2. Click **New Cycle**. In **Create Evaluation Cycle**, choose the **Quarter** (**Jan–Mar**, **Apr–Jun**, **Jul–Sep** or **Oct–Dec**), the **Year** and the **Template**. Save. The cycle is **Draft**.
3. In the cycle's actions, click **Activate**.
4. Click **Generate evaluations** to create an evaluation for each employee.
5. When the quarter's evaluations are finished, click **Close cycle** and confirm. This cannot be undone.

## How to follow all evaluations

*(HR)*

1. In the left menu, go to **People** → **All Evaluations**. **Employee Evaluations** opens.
2. Choose a cycle under **Filter by cycle**. The counters show **Total**, **Pending**, **Disputed** and **Completed**.
3. Search by name or employee ID, and open any evaluation.
4. Click **Run Auto-Resolve** to apply the 5-day rules now instead of waiting for the daily run.

## How to decide an escalated dispute

*(HR)*

1. In the left menu, go to **People** → **My Evaluation Inbox** and open the **Escalated to HR** tab, or find the evaluation in **All Evaluations**.
2. Read the dispute, the Discipline Lead's response and the **Dispute History**.
3. Under **HR Final Decision**, choose **Uphold existing score** or **Adjust final score**. If you adjust it, enter the new score.
4. Write the explanation and submit. You see *"Decision submitted"*. The status becomes **HR Decided**.

---

## How to file a finished evaluation

*(HR)*

1. Open an evaluation with status **Accepted** or **HR Decided**.
2. Click **Lock & File**.

> **After you lock it:** You see *"Evaluation locked and filed"*. The status becomes **Locked**, and nothing more can change.
