# Announcements

Announcements are company news and notices. The **Announcements** item in the left menu shows how many you have not read yet.

**Find your part of this guide**

| You are | Read these sections |
| --- | --- |
| Employee | Reading announcements and confirming that you have read one |
| Staff who can write announcements | Writing a draft |
| HR Manager and System Administrator | Publishing, scheduling, editing and checking who has read an announcement |

---

## How to read announcements

*(Employees)*

1. In the left menu, click **Announcements**.
2. Click **Unread** to see only the ones you have not read, or **All** to see everything. **Pinned** announcements stay at the top.
3. Click an announcement to open it. Download any **Attachments** at the bottom.


> **Note:** The latest announcements also appear on your dashboard under **Latest announcements**.

---

## How to confirm that you have read an announcement

*(Employees)*

Some announcements show **Acknowledgement required**.

1. Open the announcement and read it.
2. Click **I have read and understood**.

> **After you confirm:** You see *"Thank you - your acknowledgement was recorded."* The announcement now shows *"You acknowledged this on [date]."*

> **Note:** Urgent announcements that need your confirmation open in a window when you sign in, marked **Urgent - please acknowledge**. Confirm each one to continue.

## How to write a draft

*(Staff who can write announcements)*

1. In the left menu, go to **Operations** → **Manage announcements**.
2. Click **New announcement** and fill it in as described below.
3. Click **Save draft**. You see *"Draft saved."*

> **After you save:** An HR Manager reviews and publishes your draft. You can save drafts, but you cannot publish them yourself.

## How to publish an announcement

*(HR Manager and System Administrator)*

1. In the left menu, go to **Operations** → **Manage announcements**.
2. Click **New announcement**.
3. **Title** and **Message**: write the announcement. You can use bold, italic, lists and links. Click **Preview** to see how it will look, and **Write** to go back.
4. **Chinese version (optional)**: enter a **Title (Chinese)** and **Message (Chinese)**.
5. **Attachments**: click **Add files**. Recipients can download them. Each file can be up to 20 MB.
6. **Audience**: choose **Departments**, **Disciplines**, **Roles** or **Employees**. Pick at least one. The form shows how many **Recipients** will get it.
7. **Priority**: **Normal**, **Important** or **Urgent**.
8. Options:
   - **Require acknowledgement**: recipients must click *"I have read and understood"*.
   - **Send email**: also email the title, a short excerpt and a link.
   - **Pin to top**: pinned posts for all staff also reach people who join later.
9. **When to publish**: choose **Publish now**, or **Schedule** and set **Publish at**. You can also set an **Expires** date. After it, the announcement leaves the banners and dashboard but stays in the list.
10. Click **Publish now** (or **Schedule**) and confirm.


> **After you publish:** You see *"Published. Recipients: N."* A scheduled announcement shows *"Scheduled for [date]."* Click **Unschedule** to stop it.

## How to see who has read an announcement

*(HR Manager and System Administrator)*

1. On **Manage announcements**, each row shows how many have read and acknowledged it.
2. Open the announcement and go to **Read statistics**. The counters show **Recipients**, **Read** and **Acknowledged**.
3. Filter by **Not read** or **Not acknowledged**, or search by name or employee ID.

## How to edit a published announcement

*(HR Manager and System Administrator)*

1. Open the announcement on **Manage announcements** and change it.
2. Click **Save changes**.

> **Important:** The audience and delivery options cannot change after publishing. Changing the title, pin or expiry keeps everyone's acknowledgements. Changing the message, even a typo, clears them and asks everyone to read it again.

## How to post a system update

*(System Administrator)*

1. On **Manage announcements**, click **New system update**.
2. Write the update and publish it.

> **Note:** System updates go to everyone. They appear once in the **What's new** window after sign-in, with no email and no acknowledgement.
