# Announcements

Announcements are company news and notices. Everyone sees the announcements sent to them, with an unread count in the sidebar. HR Managers publish announcements, and other staff with the right access can write drafts.

**Who does what**

| Role | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Employee (everyone) | Read the announcements sent to you, and confirm when one asks for acknowledgement. |
| Staff who can write announcements | Write a draft. A draft is published by an HR Manager. |
| HR Manager and System Administrator | Write, publish, schedule, pin and edit announcements. |

**Where to find it**
- Read: sidebar → **Announcements**. The unread count shows on the item.
- Manage: sidebar → **Operations** → **Administration** → **Manage announcements** (announcement managers only).

---

## How to publish an announcement

1. Go to **Operations** → **Administration** → **Manage announcements** and click **New announcement**.
2. **Write:** enter the **Title** and the **Message**. Markdown is supported, for example **bold**, lists and links. Use **Preview** to check how it looks.
3. **Chinese version (optional):** enter a **Title (Chinese)** and **Message (Chinese)**.
4. **Attachments (optional):** add files. Recipients can download them. Up to 20 MB each.
5. **Audience:** choose **Departments**, **Disciplines**, **Roles** or **Employees**. At least one must be picked.
6. **Options:**
   - **Priority**
   - **Require acknowledgement**: recipients confirm that they have read and understood it.
   - **Send email**: also email the title, a short excerpt and a link.
   - **Pin to top**: pinned company-wide posts also reach people who join later.
7. **When to publish:** choose **Publish now**, or **Schedule** and pick the **Publish at** date and time. Optionally set an **Expires** date, after which the announcement leaves the banners and dashboard but stays in the list.
8. Click **Publish** (or **Save draft** to keep it as a draft).

> **Editing a published announcement:** the audience and delivery options cannot change. Changing the title, pin or expiry keeps everyone's acknowledgements. Changing the message clears them and asks everyone to review it again.

---

## Tips

- Check the **Recipients** count before you publish.
- Use **Require acknowledgement** for policies and notices that people must read.
