# Tips & FAQ

## Where do I start a request?
Always use **New Request** → **/approvals/new**. Only categories allowed for your account appear.

## Why is the sidebar short?
Without `hr.view` you see limited navigation (Dashboard, New Request, Approvals, Evaluation Inbox, Announcements, My Profile, and this User Guide).

## OT lunch blocked my hours
On weekdays (not holiday/site), **12:30–13:30** cannot be OT. Split before/after lunch or use a site assignment day when appropriate.

## How do I switch guide language?
Use **EN / 中文** in this portal’s header. It is stored as `help-lang` in the browser and does not change the ERP UI language.

## Why does a request type not show in New Request?
New Request only lists the request types your account is allowed to create. If you expect one and do not see it, ask HR to check your role.

## Need HR help?
Contact your HR Officer for leave balances, payroll, contracts, and account activation.
