# Tips & FAQ

Quick answers to the questions people ask most often.

---

## Where do I start a request?

Go to **New Request** in the sidebar and choose the request type. Every request type you are allowed to submit is listed there.

---

## Why does a request type not show in New Request?

**New Request** only lists the request types your account is allowed to create. If you need one that is missing, ask HR to check your role.

---

## Why is my sidebar shorter than a colleague's?

The sidebar only shows what your role can use. Most staff see Dashboard, New Request, Approvals, My Evaluation Inbox, Announcements, Expense Claims, Per Diem, My Profile and User Guide. HR staff also see the **People**, **Time & Attendance**, **Pay & Travel** and **Operations** folders.

---

## Where do I see my leave balance, overtime or payslips?

Open **My Profile**. Each tab shows one part of your record: **Leave**, **Overtime**, **Attendance**, **Payroll**, and so on.

---

## Why were my overtime hours around lunch cut?

From Monday to Friday, **12:30–13:30** is lunch and never counts as overtime. Split your entry into a part before lunch and a part after lunch. This rule does not apply on Saturdays, Sundays, public holidays or site-assignment days.

---

## My request is stuck. What can I do?

Open the request and look at the **Approval chain**. It shows which approver the request is waiting for. If that person is away or has left, ask HR. The HR Manager can reassign the step to someone else.

---

## An approver returned my request. What now?

Read the approver's note, change what they asked for, and resubmit the request from **Approvals**. The request starts again at the first approver.

---

## How do I switch the guide language?

Use the language switch (globe icon) in the top bar. It changes both the ERP interface and this User Guide.

---

## Who do I contact for help?

Contact HR for questions about your account, leave balance, payroll, contract or access.
