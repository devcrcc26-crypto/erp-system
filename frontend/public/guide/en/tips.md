# Tips & FAQ

Quick answers to the questions people ask most often.

---

## Where do I start a request?

Click **New Request** in the left menu, then click the request type you need. Every request type you are allowed to submit is listed there.

---

## Why does a request type not show in New Request?

**New Request** only lists the request types your account is allowed to create. If you need one that is missing, ask HR to check your role.

---

## Why is my left menu shorter than a colleague's?

The left menu only shows what your role can use. Most staff see **Dashboard**, **New Request**, **Approvals**, **Announcements**, **My Profile** and **User Guide**. Team and Discipline Leads also see **My Evaluation Inbox**. Anyone with people reporting to them also sees **My Team** (open a person to see their profile) and their team's **Employee Contracts** (view only), **Leave Requests**, **Overtime**, **Attendance**, **Field Movement Logs**, **Work From Home** (view only), **Site Assignments**, **All Evaluations**, **Expense Claims**, **Per Diem**, **Flight Applications**, **Third-Country Business Trips**, **Vehicle Applications** and **Exit Permit**. HR staff also see the **People**, **Time & Attendance**, **Pay & Travel** and **Operations** folders.

---

## Where do I see my leave balance, overtime or payslips?

Open **My Profile**. Each tab shows one part of your record: **Leave**, **Overtime**, **Attendance**, **Payroll**, and so on.

---

## My request is stuck. What can I do?

Open the request and look at the **Approval Chain**. It shows which approver the request is waiting for. If that person is away or has left, ask HR. HR can reassign the step to someone else.

---

## An approver returned my request. What now?

Open the request from **Approvals** → **Pending My Action** and read the approver's note. Click **Resubmit** at the top of the request. In **Resubmit request**, write a **Note to approver (optional)**, attach files if needed, and click **Resubmit**. The request starts again from the beginning of the approval chain.

---

## How do I switch the guide language?

Use the language control at the top of the screen. It changes both the ERP interface and this User Guide.

---

## Who do I contact for help?

Contact HR for questions about your account, leave balance, payroll, contract or access.
