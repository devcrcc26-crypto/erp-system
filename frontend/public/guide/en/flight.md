# Flight Applications

Use a flight application to ask for air tickets for work travel. You enter the trip and the passengers, and send it for approval. After it is approved, HR attaches the tickets and each passenger confirms they received theirs.

**Find your part of this guide**

| You are | Read these sections |
| --- | --- |
| Employee | Applying for a flight, confirming your ticket, and what to do if an application is returned |
| Anyone who approves flights | How to approve a flight application |
| HR (HR Manager, HR Assistant, HR Specialist) | Seeing all applications and attaching tickets |

## How to apply for a flight

*(Employees)*

1. In the left menu, go to **New Request**.
2. Click **Flight Application**. The form has three steps: **Trip Details**, **Passengers** and **Review & Submit**.


## Step 1 — Trip Details

*(Employees)*

1. **Trip type** (required): **One Way** or **Round Trip**.
2. **International Flight**: switch it on for a trip outside the country.
3. **Origin Country** and **Origin City** (required).
4. **Destination Country** and **Destination City** (required).
5. **Departure date** (required). It cannot be in the past.
6. **Return date** (required for a round trip): at least 1 hour 30 minutes after departure.
7. **Purpose of Travel** (required).
8. **Ticket Class** (required): **Economy** or **Business**.
9. Click **Next**.

## Step 2 — Passengers

*(Employees)*

1. **Select Passengers** (at least one): choose who is travelling. Each person can be added once.
2. For an international flight, enter for each passenger:
   - **Passport Number** (required)
   - **Visa Required**
   - **Visa Status**: **Not Applied**, **Applied** or **Approved**
3. Click **Next**.

## Step 3 — Review & Submit

*(Employees)*

1. Under **Additional Options**, you can:
   - Add **Notes** for the approver.
   - Under **Link to**, link the flight to an approved **Business Trip** or **Site assignment (optional)**. Only one link can be chosen.
   - Attach **Supporting Attachments**.
2. Check **Review Your Application**: the route, dates, ticket class, purpose and passengers.
3. Click **Back** to change anything.
4. Click **Submit Application**.


> **After you submit:** You see *"Flight application submitted"*. Your application has been sent for approval. You will receive a notification when it is approved or returned.

## How to confirm you received your ticket

*(Employees who are passengers)*

After your application is approved and HR attaches your ticket:

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** → **My Requests** and open the flight application.
2. You see *"Your ticket is ready - please acknowledge receipt"*. Open your ticket to check it.
3. Click **Acknowledge Receipt**.

> **After you confirm:** You see *"Ticket acknowledged"*.

---

## What to do if your application is returned

*(Employees)*

If your application is returned, you receive the notification **Approval returned**. Open it to see the reason.

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** → **My Requests** and open the application with status **Returned**. The banner says **This request was returned** and shows the approver's note.
2. Read the note.
3. To change the application, click **Edit** at the top of the application (or **Edit** next to it in **My Requests**). The form opens with your details filled in, and the approver's note is shown at the top. Make your changes and click **Save**. You see *"Changes saved."* and go back to the request, which is still **Returned**. You can change the trip details and passengers.
4. When you are ready, click **Resubmit** at the top of the request (if nothing needs to change, you can skip editing). In **Resubmit request**, write a **Note to approver (optional)**, click **Attach files** if needed, and click **Resubmit**.

After you resubmit, the application goes back through the approval chain from the beginning.

> **After you resubmit:** You see *"Request resubmitted"*.

> **Note:** The edit form does not take new attachments. To send a file, attach it when you click **Resubmit**.

## How to approve a flight application

*(Anyone who approves flights)*

When an application reaches you, you receive the notification **Approval required**.

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** → **Pending My Action** and open the application.
2. Check the route, dates, purpose, ticket class and passengers.
3. Choose **Approve**, **Return** (write a **Note for submitter**), or **Reject** (write the **Rejection reason**). The submitter is notified.

See the **Approvals Inbox** topic for the full steps.

## How to see all flight applications

*(HR)*

1. In the left menu, go to **Pay & Travel** → **Flight Applications**.
2. The counters show **Total**, **Pending**, **Approved** and **Rejected**. The **Needs Ticket** tab lists approved applications still waiting for tickets.
3. Search by application number or route, or filter by **Employee**.
4. The columns show **Application #**, **Submitted by**, **Route**, **Departure**, **Trip Type**, **International**, **Status** and **Current Step**.
5. Click an application to open it.

## How to attach tickets

*(HR Manager — approved applications only)*

When an application is approved, its page shows **Tickets required**.

1. Open the application.
2. Choose one:
   - **Combined Ticket**: click **Upload Combined Ticket** to attach one ticket that covers every passenger.
   - **Individual Tickets**: click **Upload Ticket** on each passenger's row.
3. To change a ticket, click **Replace**. To remove it, click **Remove** and confirm. Removing a ticket resets that passenger's confirmation.
4. The **Acknowledged** column shows **Pending** until the passenger confirms, then **Acknowledged**.

## Things HR should know

*(HR)*

- **Past departure dates.** Only HR officers can enter a departure date in the past.
- **Passenger import.** On a **Draft** application, **Import from Excel** in the passengers section adds many passengers at once.
- **Cancelling.** Only the person who submitted an application can cancel it, while it is **Draft** or **Submitted**.
