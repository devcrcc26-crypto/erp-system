# Flight Applications

Use a flight application to request an air ticket for business travel. You fill in the trip, add the passengers, review, and submit. The request goes into the approval chain.

**Who does what**

| Role | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Employee (everyone) | Submit a flight application for yourself, or for someone else if you are allowed to submit on behalf. Track it in **Approvals**. |
| Approvers | Approve the request when it reaches you in **Approvals**. |
| HR (HR Manager, HR Assistant, HR Specialist) | See all flight applications by clicking **Pay & Travel** in the left menu, then **Flight Applications** and process them. |

**Where to find it**
- Submit: click **New Request** in the left menu, then click **Flight**.
- HR list: click **Pay & Travel** in the left menu, then click **Flight Applications** (HR only).

---

## How to submit a flight application

1. Click **New Request** in the left menu, then click **Flight**.
2. **Submit on behalf (optional):** if you are submitting for someone else, select the employee first. The form requires this when you submit on behalf.
3. **Trip Details:**
   - Enter the **Origin country** and **Origin city**.
   - Enter the **Destination country** and **Destination city**.
   - Enter the **Departure date**.
   - For a round trip, enter the **Return date**. It must be on or after the departure date, and at least 1 hour 30 minutes after departure.
   - Enter the **Purpose**.
4. **Passengers:** add at least one passenger. Each passenger can only be added once. If a passenger needs a passport number for the trip, the form asks for it.
5. Click **Review & Submit**, check the details, and click **Submit Application**.

> **What happens next:** The application enters the approval chain. You can see its status in **Approvals**. HR can see every application in the HR list.

---

## Tips

- Check the passenger names exactly as they appear on the passport. Missing passport numbers stop the submission.
- A one-way trip has no return date. Leave it empty.

---

## Things that stop a submission

- Missing origin or destination country or city.
- A return date before the departure date, or less than 1 hour 30 minutes after it.
- No passengers, or the same passenger added twice.
- A required passport number that is blank.
