# Site Assignments

A site assignment records the days an employee works at a project site, where there is no attendance machine. Those days count as paid attendance, and overtime on them can be claimed for the whole day. A supervisor or HR creates the assignment, and it is active straight away. There is no approval step.

## How to see your site assignments

*(Employees)*

Your supervisor or HR creates site assignments for you. You cannot create one for yourself.

1. In the left menu, go to **My Profile**.
2. Click the **Leave** tab.
3. Scroll to **Site assignments**. Each row shows **Request #**, **Site**, **Start Date**, **End Date**, **Days** and **Status**.


> **Note:** If a site assignment is missing or wrong, ask your supervisor or HR to correct it.

## How to create a site assignment for your team

*(Supervisors — for people who report directly to you)*

1. In the left menu, go to **New Request**.
2. Click **Site Assignment**. The **Site assignment request** form opens. It has two steps: **Details** and **Review**.


## Step 1 — Details

*(Supervisors)*

1. **Employee** (required): choose the team member. Only people who report directly to you are listed.
2. **Site name** (required): for example *Dire Dawa*.
3. **Project (optional)**: the project name.
4. **Periods** (at least one is required): pick the first and last day of each period. The form shows the number of calendar days.
   - Click **Add period** for dates that are not next to each other. Each period becomes its own record.
   - Click **Remove period** to delete one.
   - Periods must not overlap.
5. **Late entry reason**: appears if a period is in an earlier month. Write why the dates are being entered late (at least 3 characters). It is required.
6. **Reason** (optional): any other notes.
7. Click **Next**.

> **Note:** The dates follow the payroll lock rules. You cannot choose dates in a month that payroll has already locked.

## Step 2 — Review

*(Supervisors)*

1. Check the employee, site, periods and days.
2. Click **Back** to change anything.
3. Click **Create assignment**.

> **After you create it:** You see *"Site assignment is now active."* (or *"N site assignments submitted."* for several periods). The assignment takes effect immediately. The employee sees it on their profile.

### Why the form might not let you create it

- *"Choose the employee this site assignment is for."*: no employee was selected.
- *"Select at least one date range."* or *"Periods must not overlap."*: check the periods.
- The dates overlap another site assignment, leave or work from home for the same employee.

## How to create a site assignment for a supervisor's team

*(HR)*

1. In the left menu, go to **New Request** and click **Site Assignment**, or go to **Time & Attendance** → **Site Assignments** and click **New site assignment**.
2. **Supervisor** (required): choose the supervisor the assignment is filed for.
3. **Employee** (required): choose one of that supervisor's direct reports.
4. Fill in the rest of **Details** as described above. HR can enter dates in earlier months with a **Late entry reason**.
5. Click **Next**, check the **Review**, and click **Create assignment**.

## How to see all site assignments

*(HR)*

1. In the left menu, go to **Time & Attendance** → **Site Assignments**.
2. The counters show **All requests**, **Pending**, **Approved** and **Rejected / cancelled**.
3. Search by employee, site or request number, or filter by **Site name** and **Month**. Click **Reset** to clear the filters.
4. The columns show **Request #**, **Employee**, **Site**, **Project**, **Start**, **End**, **Days** and **Status**.
5. Click a row to open the assignment. **Assignment details** shows the dates, project and reason, and **Linked per diem advances** shows any per diem paid for it.

## How to shorten a site assignment

*(HR — approved assignments only)*

Use this when the employee came back from site early.

1. Open the assignment.
2. Click **Shorten end date**. The **Shorten site assignment** window opens.
3. **New end date** (required): it must be on or after the start date, and before the current end date.
4. **Reason** (required).
5. Confirm.

> **After you confirm:** You see *"End date updated and attendance recalculated."*

## How to cancel a site assignment

*(HR — approved assignments only)*

1. Open the assignment.
2. Click **Cancel assignment**. The **Cancel approved site assignment** window opens.
3. **Reason** (required).
4. Confirm.

> **After you confirm:** You see *"Site assignment updated."* The status becomes **Cancelled**, and attendance for those days is recalculated.

## Things HR should know

*(HR)*

- **No approval step.** Site assignments are approved as soon as they are created.
- **Supervisors only file for direct reports.** A supervisor cannot file a site assignment for themselves or for someone outside their team. HR can file for any supervisor's team.
- **Attendance and overtime.** Site assignment days count as attended. On those days the whole day can be claimed as overtime, and the weekday lunch rule does not apply.
- **Unlock attendance.** If you add or change a site assignment for a month whose attendance is already approved, unlock and re-approve that month so the figures update.
