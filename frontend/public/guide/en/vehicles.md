# Vehicles

This topic covers three requests: booking a company vehicle (**Vehicle Application**), asking for fuel for an office vehicle (**Office Vehicle Refueling**), and reporting a device or vehicle that needs inspection, maintenance or repair (**Inspection and Repair**). It also covers the company vehicle register.

**Find your part of this guide**

| You are | Read these sections |
| --- | --- |
| Employee | Booking a vehicle, requesting fuel, reporting a repair, and what to do if a request is returned |
| Anyone who approves vehicle requests | How to approve a vehicle or refueling request |
| Fleet and HR staff | Seeing all requests, releasing vehicles, uploading fuel receipts, updating repairs and managing company vehicles |

## How to book a company vehicle

*(Employees)*

1. In the left menu, go to **New Request**.
2. Click **Vehicle Application**. The form has four steps: **Trip Schedule**, **Select Vehicle**, **Trip Details** and **Review & Submit**.


## Step 1 — Trip Schedule

*(Employees)*

1. **Departure Date & Time** (required): it cannot be in the past.
2. **Return Date & Time** (required): it must be after the departure.
3. Click **Next**.

> **Note:** If you change the dates later, the vehicle you picked is cleared and you pick again.

## Step 2 — Select Vehicle

*(Employees)*

1. The vehicles free for your dates are shown as cards. A faded card is already booked. Point at it to see which application it *"Conflicts with"*.
2. Click the vehicle you want. Its driver, if it has one, is shown on the card.
3. Click **Next**.


## Step 3 — Trip Details

*(Employees)*

1. **Drive Type** (required):
   - **Company Driver**: the vehicle's assigned driver drives. The **Driver** field fills in automatically. You can only choose this when the vehicle has a driver.
   - **Self Drive**: you drive yourself.
2. **Destination** (required): city and country.
3. **Number of Passengers**: 1 to 50.
4. **Purpose of Travel** (required).
5. **Passenger Names**: one name per line.
6. **Notes**: anything else your approver should know.
7. Click **Next**.

## Step 4 — Review & Submit

*(Employees)*

1. Check **Review Your Application**: departure, return, vehicle, destination, passengers and purpose.
2. Click **Back** to change anything.
3. Click **Submit Application**.

> **After you submit:** You see *"Vehicle application submitted for approval"*. Your request has been sent for approval. You will receive a notification when it is approved or returned.

### Why the form might not let you submit

- *"Select a vehicle"*: no vehicle was picked in Step 2.
- *"Selected vehicle has no assigned company driver. Choose Self Drive or pick another vehicle."*
- *"Return must be after departure"*.

## How to request fuel for an office vehicle

*(Employees)*

1. In the left menu, go to **New Request**.
2. Click **Office Vehicle Refueling**. The form has three steps: **Select Vehicle**, **Request Details** and **Review & Submit**.


## Step 1 — Select Vehicle

*(Employees)*

1. Choose the company vehicle.
2. **Driver** (optional): choose the driver.
3. Click **Next**.

## Step 2 — Request Details

*(Employees)*

1. **Fuel Date** (required).
2. **Fuel Type** (required): **Petrol**, **Diesel**, **Electric** or **Hybrid**. It starts as **Diesel**.
3. **Fuel Station** (required): the name of the station.
4. **Current Mileage (km)**.
5. **Requested Litres** and **Unit Price (ETB/L)**. The **Total Cost (ETB)** is worked out for you.
6. **Notes**.
7. Click **Next**.

## Step 3 — Review & Submit

*(Employees)*

1. Check the vehicle, fuel details and total cost.
2. Click **Back** to change anything.
3. Click **Submit Request**.

> **After you submit:** You see *"Refueling request submitted for approval"*. Your request has been sent for approval. You will receive a notification when it is approved or returned.

## How to report an inspection, maintenance or repair

*(Employees)*

1. In the left menu, go to **New Request**.
2. Click **Inspection and Repair**. The form has three steps: **Asset**, **Request Details** and **Review & Submit**.


## Step 1 — Asset

*(Employees)*

1. **Asset Source** (required):
   - **Use assigned company asset**: pick one of the assets assigned to you.
   - **Enter device manually**: enter the **Device Name** (required), **Make**, **Model** and **Year**.
2. If you have no assets assigned, you see *"No assigned company assets found. Enter the device details manually."*
3. Click **Next**.

## Step 2 — Request Details

*(Employees)*

1. **Location** (required): where the device is.
2. **Inspection Type** (required): **Inspection**, **Maintenance** or **Repair**.
3. **Urgency** (required): **Critical**, **Urgent**, **Medium** or **Minor**.
4. **Description** (required): describe what is wrong.
5. **Suggested Solution** (optional): your idea for the fix.
6. Click **Next**.

## Step 3 — Review & Submit

*(Employees)*

1. Check the asset and request details.
2. Click **Back** to change anything.
3. Click **Submit Request**.

> **After you submit:** You see *"Inspection and repair request submitted"*. This request has no approval step, so it does not appear in **Approvals**. It goes straight to the people who arrange the work.

## How to follow a vehicle or refueling request

*(Employees)*

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** → **My Requests** and open the request.
2. You see the details, the status and the **Approval Chain**.

## What to do if your request is returned

*(Employees — Vehicle Application and Office Vehicle Refueling)*

If your request is returned, you receive the notification **Approval returned**. Open it to see the reason.

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** → **My Requests** and open the request with status **Returned**. The banner says **This request was returned** and shows the approver's note.
2. Read the note.
3. To change the request, click **Edit** at the top of the request (or **Edit** next to it in **My Requests**). The form opens with your details filled in, and the approver's note is shown at the top. Make your changes and click **Save**. You see *"Changes saved."* and go back to the request, which is still **Returned**. The vehicle's availability is checked again for the new dates.
4. When you are ready, click **Resubmit** at the top of the request (if nothing needs to change, you can skip editing). In **Resubmit request**, write a **Note to approver (optional)**, click **Attach files** if needed, and click **Resubmit**.

After you resubmit, the request goes back through the approval chain from the beginning.

> **After you resubmit:** You see *"Request resubmitted"*.

> **Note:** **Edit** is available for **Vehicle Application** only. **Office Vehicle Refueling** cannot be edited: click **Resubmit** and explain the change in your note.

## How to approve a vehicle or refueling request

*(Anyone who approves vehicle requests)*

When a request reaches you, you receive the notification **Approval required**.

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** → **Pending My Action** and open the request.
2. For a vehicle application, check the dates, vehicle, drive type, destination, passengers and purpose. For refueling, check the vehicle, fuel date, litres and total cost.
3. Choose **Approve**, **Return** (write a **Note for submitter**), or **Reject** (write the **Rejection reason**). The submitter is notified.

> **Note:** From the moment a vehicle application is submitted, that vehicle is held for the trip dates and nobody else can book it. If you return or reject the application, the vehicle becomes free again.

## How to see all vehicle requests

*(Fleet and HR staff)*

1. In the left menu, go to **Operations** → **Fleet & Assets**, and click **Vehicle Applications**, **Vehicle Refueling** or **Inspection & Repair**.
2. The counters show the totals. Vehicle applications and refueling show **Total**, **Draft**, **Pending**, **Approved** and **Rejected**. Inspection & Repair shows **Total**, **Pending**, **In repair** and **Completed**.
3. Search, or filter by **Employee**. Click a request to open it.
4. Click **Export applications** or **Export requests** to download the list. **Import from Excel** creates **Draft** requests. Nothing is submitted or approved, so review each draft and submit it.

## How to release a vehicle after the trip

*(Fleet and HR staff — approved vehicle applications only)*

1. Open the approved application in **Vehicle Applications**.
2. Click **Release Vehicle** and confirm. *"The vehicle will be marked as available for other bookings."*

> **After you release it:** You see *"Vehicle released successfully"*. The application shows **Released** and **Released At**, and the vehicle is **Available** again.

## How to upload a fuel receipt

*(Fleet and HR staff — approved refueling requests only)*

1. Open the approved request in **Vehicle Refueling**. It shows *"This request has been approved. Please upload the fuel receipt."*
2. Click **Upload Receipt** and choose the file.

> **After you upload:** You see *"Receipt uploaded successfully"*. The request shows **Receipt attached**.

## How to update an inspection or repair

*(Fleet and HR staff)*

1. Open the request in **Inspection & Repair**.
2. Click **Update Status**. The **Update request status** window shows the **Current status**.
3. Choose the **New status**:
   - From **Submitted**: **In Repair** (work has started) or **Rejected** (close it without repair).
   - From **In Repair**: **Repaired** (work is complete) or **Rejected**.
4. Confirm.

> **After you update:** You see *"Inspection and repair request updated"*. When it is **Repaired** or **Rejected**, you see *"No further status changes are available for this request."*

## How to add a company vehicle

*(HR)*

1. In the left menu, go to **Operations** → **Fleet & Assets** → **Company Vehicles**.
2. Click **Add vehicle**. The form has five steps:
   - **Vehicle identity**: **Plate Number**, **Vehicle type** and **Fuel type**.
   - **Specifications**: **Make**, **Model**, **Year** and **Color**.
   - **Assignment & notes**: **Assigned Driver** (only people with the Driver position are listed), **Active in fleet** and **Notes**.
   - **Vehicle photo**: click **Choose photo** to add a JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF up to 5 MB. You can skip this step.
   - **Review & submit**: check everything.
3. Click **Create vehicle**.


> **Note:** Only vehicles with an **Assigned Driver** can be booked with **Company Driver**.

## How to change, update or remove a company vehicle

*(HR)*

1. On **Company Vehicles**, the counters show **Total vehicles**, **Available**, **In use** and **Maintenance / retired**.
2. On a vehicle, open the actions and choose:
   - **Update** to change its details, then click **Save changes**.
   - **Change status** to set a new status, for example **Maintenance** or **Retired**. **Available** and **In Use** are set automatically by bookings.
   - **Delete** to remove it. Confirm **Delete vehicle?**. This cannot be undone.
3. Click **Export vehicles** to download the register, or **Import from Excel** to add many vehicles at once. Imported vehicles are **Available**, and plate numbers must be unique.

## Things HR should know

*(Fleet and HR staff)*

- **Past departure dates.** Only HR staff can book a vehicle with a departure date in the past.
- **Cancelling.** Only the person who made a request can cancel it, while it is **Draft** or **Submitted**. They click **Cancel Request** and confirm.
- **Approved directly.** A request with *"Approved directly - no approval required"* had no approvers.
