# Vehicles

Vehicle requests cover three things: applying to use a company vehicle, recording fuel for an office vehicle, and reporting an inspection, maintenance or repair for a device or vehicle. Each one is its own request type.

**Who does what**

| Role | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Employee (everyone) | Apply for a vehicle, record refueling, or report an inspection or repair if your account is allowed to create it. Track it in **Approvals**. |
| Approvers | Approve the request when it reaches you in **Approvals**. |
| Fleet and HR staff | See all vehicle requests by clicking **Operations** in the left menu, then **Vehicle Applications**, **Vehicle Refueling** or **Inspection & Repair**, and process them. |

**Where to find it**
- Submit: **New Request**, then choose **Vehicle Application**, **Vehicle Refueling** or **Inspection & Repair**.
- Lists: click **Operations** in the left menu, then click **Vehicle Applications**, **Vehicle Refueling** or **Inspection & Repair** (fleet access only).
- Company vehicles register: click **Operations** in the left menu, then click **Company Vehicles** (HR only).

---

## How to report an inspection, maintenance or repair

1. Click **New Request** in the left menu, then click **Inspection & Repair**.
2. **Device:** choose **Use assigned company asset** and pick the asset, or choose **Enter device manually** and enter the **Device Name**, **Make**, **Model** and **Year**.
3. **Request Details:** enter the **Location**, the **Inspection Type** (*Inspection*, *Maintenance* or *Repair*), the **Urgency** (*Critical*, *Urgent*, *Medium* or *Minor*), a **Description** of the issue, and an optional **Suggested Solution**.
4. Click **Review & Submit**, check the details, and click **Submit Request**.

> **What happens next:** The request enters the approval chain and you can follow it in **Approvals**.

---

## How to apply for a vehicle and record refueling

- **Vehicle Application:** click **New Request** in the left menu, then click **Vehicle Application**. Enter the trip details and submit.
- **Vehicle Refueling:** click **New Request** in the left menu, then click **Vehicle Refueling**. Enter the vehicle, date and fuel details, and submit.

Follow the steps on each form. The **Review & Submit** step shows the summary before you send it.
