# Approvals Inbox

**Approvals** is where you start requests, follow the requests you sent, and decide on requests that are waiting for you.

**Find your part of this guide**

| You are | Read these sections |
| --- | --- |
| Employee | Starting a request, following your requests, drafts, conditions, and what to do if a request is returned |
| Anyone who approves (Team Lead, Department Head, Discipline Lead, HR, General Manager, Managing Director) | Finding requests that wait for you, and approving, returning, rejecting or conditionally approving them |
| HR Manager, System Administrator | Reassigning a waiting step |
| General Manager, Managing Director, HR Manager | Approving or returning on behalf of another approver, and reports |

---

## How to start a new request

*(Everyone)*

1. In the left menu, go to **New Request**. The page **What would you like to request?** opens.
2. Find the request type:
   - Type in **Search request types**, or
   - Click a group, such as **Time & attendance**, **Travel** or **Expenses & allowances**, or
   - Pick one from **Recently used**.
3. Click the request type. Its form opens.
4. Fill in the form and submit it. Each request type has its own topic in this guide with the exact steps.


> **After you submit:** Your request has been sent for approval. You will receive a notification when it is approved or returned.

---

## How to follow your requests

*(Everyone)*

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals**.
2. Click the **My Requests** tab.
3. The counters at the top show **Total**, **Pending**, **Approved** and **Rejected**.
4. Search by title or request number, or filter by **Category**. Click **Reset** to clear the filters.
5. The columns show **Request #**, **Title**, **Category**, **Submitted**, **Status** and **Current tier**.
6. Click a request to open it. The right side shows the **Approval Chain** and what the request is waiting for, for example *"Waiting on …"* or *"Fully approved - no further action required"*.


**What the statuses mean**

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Submitted | Sent and waiting for the first approver. |
| In Review | An approver is reviewing it. |
| Returned | Sent back to you to change. |
| Approved | Fully approved. |
| Rejected | Not approved. The request is closed. |
| Cancelled | Cancelled. It stays in your **My Requests** history, read-only, but is removed from approvers' lists. |

## How to continue a draft

*(Everyone)*

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** and click the **My Drafts** tab.
2. The list shows **Request #**, **Title**, **Category**, **Last Modified** and **Status**. Use **All modules** to filter by request type.
3. Click a draft to continue filling it in, then submit it.

## What to do if your request is returned

*(Everyone who submits requests)*

If your request is returned, you receive the notification **Approval returned**. Open the request to see the reason.

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** → **Pending My Action** and open the request with status **Returned**. The banner says **This request was returned** and shows the approver's note.
2. Read the note.
3. If the request needs to change, click **Edit** at the top of the request (or **Edit** next to it in **My Requests**). The form opens with your details filled in, and the approver's note is shown at the top. Make your changes and click **Save**. You see *"Changes saved."* and go back to the request, which is still **Returned**. Saving does not send it for approval.
   - For leave, click **Edit** in the request details on the same page.
   - For overtime, **Edit** opens the overtime sheet. Change the entries there, then submit the sheet again from the sheet page. Overtime has no **Resubmit** button in **Approvals**.
4. When you are ready, click **Resubmit** at the top of the request. If nothing needs to change, you can skip step 3.
5. In **Resubmit request**, write a **Note to approver (optional)** to explain what you changed. Click **Attach files** to add files if needed, then click **Resubmit**. Click **Cancel** to close it without resubmitting.

The request goes back through the approval chain from the beginning, with the details as they are now.

> **After you resubmit:** You see *"Request resubmitted"*. Your note is shown to approvers in **Resubmission / response history**, together with earlier notes.

> **Drafts:** a request you saved but never submitted is a draft. Go to **Approvals** → **My Drafts** and click **Edit** next to it. Saving a draft keeps it as a draft (*"Changes saved."*).

> You can only edit your own requests, and only while they are a draft or returned. Once a request is pending, approved or completed, **Edit** is not shown.

## How to respond to a condition

*(Everyone who submits requests)*

An approver can approve your request on a condition. You then receive a notification.

1. Open the request in **Approvals** → **My Requests**.
2. Read the **Condition**.
3. Under **Your response**, write your answer. Click **Attach files** to add files if needed.
4. Click **Submit response**. The request shows *"Response submitted - awaiting review"* until the approver marks the condition resolved.

## How to find requests waiting for you

*(Anyone who approves)*

When a request reaches your step, you receive the notification **Approval required**. The **Approvals** item in the left menu shows how many requests are waiting.

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals**.
2. Click the **Pending My Action** tab.
3. The **Action needed** column shows what to do. Search by title or request number, or filter by **Category**.
4. Click a request to open it.

## How to check a request before you decide

*(Anyone who approves)*

1. Read the request details and attachments. The heading shows *"Submitted by … on …"*.
2. If the request was resubmitted, you see *"Resubmitted N time(s)"*. Read the **Resubmission / response history**.
3. Look at the **Approval Chain** on the right. The step marked **Current** is waiting now.

> **Note:** You can only decide while the request is at your step. You see the decision buttons under **Decide**.

## How to approve a request

*(Anyone who approves)*

1. Click **Approve**.
2. **Note (optional)**: add a note.
3. Click **Approve**.

> **After you approve:** You see *"Tier approved"*. If there is another approver, the request moves to them. If you were the last approver, the request is **Approved** and the employee is notified.

## How to return a request

*(Anyone who approves)*

Return a request when the employee can fix it.

1. Click **Return**.
2. **Note for submitter**: say exactly what to change.
3. Click **Return**.

> **After you return it:** You see *"Request returned to submitter"*. The employee is notified, changes the request and resubmits it. It then starts again at the first approver.

## How to reject a request

*(Anyone who approves)*

Reject a request only when it should not go ahead at all.

1. Click **Reject**.
2. **Rejection reason**: write the reason. The employee sees it.
3. Click **Reject**. This cannot be undone.

> **After you reject it:** You see *"Request rejected"*. The request is closed and the employee is notified.

## How to approve on a condition

*(Anyone who approves)*

Use this when you agree in principle but something must be confirmed first.

1. Click **Conditionally Approve**.
2. **Condition** (required): write what must be resolved.
3. Click **Submit**.
4. The request stays at your step. When the employee responds, you see *"Employee responded - ready to resolve"*.
5. Click **Resolve** when the condition is met. Then approve, return or reject the request as usual.

> **Note:** While a condition is open, you see *"Resolve all conditions before taking action."* and cannot approve, return or reject.

## How to reassign a waiting step

*(HR Manager, System Administrator)*

Use this when an approver is away or has left.

1. Open the request.
2. In the **Approval Chain**, click **Reassign** on the step that is waiting.
3. **New approver** (required): choose an active employee who can approve.
4. **Reason** (required, at least 3 characters): say why.
5. Click **Reassign**. The new approver is notified.

## How to approve or return on behalf of another approver

*(General Manager, Managing Director, HR Manager, System Administrator)*

If a request is waiting at someone else's step, you see **Approve on behalf of …** and **Return on behalf of …**.

1. Click **Approve on behalf of …** or **Return on behalf of …**.
2. Read the window. It names the step and the approver you are acting for.
3. Click **Approve on behalf** or **Return on behalf**.

The original approver is notified, and the step records that you acted for them. You cannot act on behalf on your own request or on a request you filed for someone.

## How to see every request and company reports

*(General Manager, Managing Director, HR)*

- **All requests:** in **Approvals**, click the **All Requests** tab to see every request in the company. Use the search and **Category** filter.
- **Dashboard:** in the left menu, click **Dashboard**. The **Overview**, **Payroll & OT** and **Operations** views summarize the company.
- **HR reports:** in the left menu, go to **People** → **HR Reports**. The tabs are **Overview**, **Leave & OT**, **Payroll & spend** and **Contracts & attendance**.
