# Business Trips

Use a business trip request for travel to a third country. You enter the trip, its purpose and budget, and the travellers, and send it for approval. After approval, HR attaches the tickets and each traveller confirms they received theirs.

| Employee | Requesting a business trip, confirming your ticket, and what to do if a request is returned |

## How to request a business trip

*(Employees)*

1. In the left menu, go to **New Request**.
2. Click **Third-Country Business Trip**. The form has four steps: **Trip Details**, **Purpose & Budget**, **Passengers** and **Review & Submit**.


## Step 1 — Trip Details

*(Employees)*

1. **Trip type** (required): **One Way** or **Round Trip**.
2. **Origin Country** and **Origin City** (required).
3. **Destination Country** and **Destination City** (required).
4. **Departure date** (required). It cannot be in the past.
5. **Return date** (required for a round trip): at least 1 hour 30 minutes after departure.
6. **Ticket Class** (required).
7. Click **Next**.

## Step 2 — Purpose & Budget

*(Employees)*

1. **Purpose of Travel** (required).
2. **Host organization**: the **Organization name**.
3. **Host contact person**: **Contact name**, **Phone** and **Email**. The email must be a valid address.
4. **Expected Outcomes** and **Itinerary**.
5. **Budget Estimate** in ETB: **Estimated Accommodation Cost**, **Estimated Per Diem** and **Estimated Other Costs**. The **Estimated Total** is added up for you.
6. Click **Next**.

## Step 3 — Passengers

*(Employees)*

1. **Select Passengers** (at least one): each person can be added once.
2. For each passenger, enter the **Passport Number** (required), **Visa Required** and **Visa Status** (**Not Applied**, **Applied** or **Approved**).
3. Click **Next**.

## Step 4 — Review & Submit

*(Employees)*

1. Under **Additional Options**, add **Notes** and attach **Supporting Attachments** if needed.
2. Check the summary.
3. Click **Back** to change anything.
4. Click **Submit Business Trip**.


> **After you submit:** You see *"Business trip submitted"*. Your request has been sent for approval. You will receive a notification when it is approved or returned.

## How to confirm you received your ticket

*(Employees who are travelling)*

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** → **My Requests** and open the business trip.
2. You see *"Your ticket is ready - please acknowledge receipt"*. Open your ticket to check it.
3. Click **Acknowledge Receipt**.

> **After you confirm:** You see *"Ticket acknowledged"*.

---

## What to do if your request is returned

*(Employees)*

If your request is returned, you receive the notification **Approval returned**. Open it to see the reason.

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** → **My Requests** and open the request with status **Returned**. The banner says **This request was returned** and shows the approver's note.
2. Read the note.
3. To change the request, click **Edit** at the top of the request (or **Edit** next to it in **My Requests**). The form opens with your details filled in, and the approver's note is shown at the top. Make your changes and click **Save**. You see *"Changes saved."* and go back to the request, which is still **Returned**. You can change the trip details, purpose and budget, and passengers.
4. When you are ready, click **Resubmit** at the top of the request (if nothing needs to change, you can skip editing). In **Resubmit request**, write a **Note to approver (optional)**, click **Attach files** if needed, and click **Resubmit**.

After you resubmit, the request goes back through the approval chain from the beginning.

> **After you resubmit:** You see *"Request resubmitted"*.

> **Note:** The edit form does not take new attachments. To send a file, attach it when you click **Resubmit**.

## How to approve a business trip

*(Anyone who approves business trips)*

When a request reaches you, you receive the notification **Approval required**.

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** → **Pending My Action** and open the request.
2. Check the route, dates, purpose, host, budget and passengers.
3. Choose **Approve**, **Return** (write a **Note for submitter**), or **Reject** (write the **Rejection reason**). The submitter is notified.

See the **Approvals Inbox** topic for the full steps.

## How to see all business trips

*(HR)*

1. In the left menu, go to **Pay & Travel** → **Third-Country Business Trips**.
2. Use the counters and the **Needs Ticket** tab to find trips waiting for tickets.
3. Search, or filter by employee, and click a trip to open it.

## How to attach tickets

*(HR Manager — approved trips only)*

1. Open the approved trip. It shows **Tickets required**.
2. Click **Upload Combined Ticket** for one ticket covering everyone, or **Upload Ticket** on each passenger's row.
3. Click **Replace** to change a ticket, or **Remove** to delete it. Removing a ticket resets that passenger's confirmation.
4. The **Acknowledged** column shows **Pending** until each passenger confirms.

## Things HR should know

*(HR)*

- **Past departure dates.** Only HR officers can enter a departure date in the past.
- **Passenger import.** On a **Draft** trip, **Import from Excel** adds many passengers at once.
- **Cancelling.** Only the person who submitted a trip can cancel it, while it is **Draft** or **Submitted**.
