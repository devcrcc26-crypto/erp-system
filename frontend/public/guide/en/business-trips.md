# Business Trips

Use a business trip request for travel that needs approval as a trip: origin, destination, dates, passengers, purpose and budget, and a host contact. The request is sent to your approvers.

**Who does what**

| Role | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Employee (everyone) | Submit a business trip for yourself, or for someone else if you are allowed to submit on behalf. Track it in **Approvals**. |
| Approvers | Approve the request when it reaches you in **Approvals**. |
| HR (HR Manager, HR Assistant, HR Specialist) | See all business trips by clicking **Pay & Travel** in the left menu, then **Third-Country Business Trips** and process them. |

**Where to find it**
- Submit: click **New Request** in the left menu, then click **Business Trip**.
- HR list: click **Pay & Travel** in the left menu, then click **Third-Country Business Trips** (HR only).

---

## How to submit a business trip

1. Click **New Request** in the left menu, then click **Business Trip**.
2. **Submit on behalf (optional):** if you are submitting for someone else, select the employee first.
3. **Trip Details:**
   - Enter the **Origin country** and **Origin city**.
   - Enter the **Destination country** and **Destination city**.
   - Enter the **Departure date**. For a round trip, enter the **Return date**, which must be at least 1 hour 30 minutes after departure.
4. **Purpose & Budget:**
   - Enter the **Purpose**.
   - Enter the **host contact** details. The host contact email must be a valid email address.
5. **Passengers:** add at least one passenger. Each passenger can only be added once. Enter a passport number where the form asks for it.
6. Click **Review & Submit**, check the details, and click **Submit Business Trip**.

> **What happens next:** The trip is sent to your approvers. Track its status in **Approvals**.

---

## Things that stop a submission

- Missing origin or destination country or city.
- A return date that is not at least 1 hour 30 minutes after departure.
- No passengers, or the same passenger added twice.
- A missing purpose, or an invalid host contact email.
- A required passport number that is blank.
