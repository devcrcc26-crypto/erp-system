# Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first page you see after signing in. It gives you shortcuts to common requests and a summary of the work that matters to your role.

**Who sees what**

| Role | Dashboard views |
| --- | --- |
| Everyone | **My Work** |
| Team Lead, Department Head, Discipline Lead, and anyone with people reporting to them | **My Team** and **My Work** |
| HR, General Manager, Managing Director, System Administrator | **Overview**, **Payroll & OT** and **Operations**, plus **My Team** if they lead a team, and **My Work** |

**Where to find it**
- Sidebar → **Dashboard** (the first item). You also land here after you sign in.

---

## Quick actions

The buttons at the top of the Dashboard open the most common pages:

- **New leave**
- **New overtime**
- **New per diem**
- **New flight**
- **Approvals inbox**
- **My profile**

You only see the request buttons your account is allowed to use.

---

## The Dashboard views

Click a view name to switch between views. The ERP remembers the open view when you reload the page.

| View | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **My Work** | Your own items, such as your leave balance, overtime, site time and requests in progress. |
| **My Team** | A summary for the people who report to you. |
| **Overview** | A company-wide summary for HR and management. |
| **Payroll & OT** | Company payroll and overtime figures. |
| **Operations** | Company-wide operational requests and activity. |

---

## Tips

- The figures always match what your role is allowed to see. A view never shows records you cannot open elsewhere.
- Use the quick actions to start a request without going through **New Request**.
