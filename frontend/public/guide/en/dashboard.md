# Dashboard

The **Dashboard** is the first page you see after signing in. It shows shortcuts to common requests and a summary of the work that matters to you. What you see depends on your role.

**Find your part of this guide**

| You are | Read these sections |
| --- | --- |
| Employee | Quick actions and **your personal summary** (shown on the page — there is no **My Work** tab) |
| Anyone with people reporting to them | **My Team** (and **My Work** when more than one view is available) |
| HR, General Manager, Managing Director, System Administrator | **Overview**, **Payroll & OT**, **Operations** and **My Work** |

---

## How to start a request from the Dashboard

*(Everyone)*

1. In the left menu, click **Dashboard**.
2. On the header of the dashboard, click one of the buttons. You only see the ones your account can use:
   - **New leave**
   - **New overtime**
   - **New per diem**
   - **New flight**
   - **Approvals inbox**
   - **My profile**


---

## Your personal summary (leave, overtime, requests)

*(Everyone)*

If you are a normal employee, the Dashboard opens straight onto your personal summary. You do **not** see a **My Work** tab, that tab only appears when your role also has other views (for example **My Team** or company **Overview**).

On this summary you see:

- **My snapshot**: your leave balance, overtime this month, your pending requests and your next site assignment.
- **My pending approvals**: requests that need your decision, and how long they have waited.
- **My requests**: requests you have sent that are still in progress.
- **This month's attendance**: days present, absent and holidays.
- **Overtime this month**: total hours and your sheet's status. Click **Open this month's sheet** to go to it.
- **Leave balance**: days left by leave type.
- **My evaluation**: your current quarterly evaluation, and whether it needs you.

If you do see tabs at the top, click **My Work** to open the same summary. Click **View all** on any card to see the full list.

## What My Team shows

*(Anyone with people reporting to them)*

Click **My Team**. You see your **Direct reports**, the requests **Pending my approval**, your team's overtime this month, who is on leave, and team site assignments that are ending soon.

> **Note:** You see **My Team** only if at least one person reports to you.

## What the company views show

*(HR, General Manager, Managing Director, System Administrator)*

- **Overview**: headcount, people on leave today, new joiners this month, pending approvals across the company, leave by type, contracts ending in the next 90 days, and the status of this month's attendance review.
- **Payroll & OT**: overtime hours this month, overtime by employee, per diem waiting for settlement or approval, and the status of this month's payroll run. Click **Open payroll** to go to it.
- **Operations**: company-wide requests and per diem activity.

> **Note:** The Dashboard remembers which view you had open, even after you reload the page. Figures always match what your role can open elsewhere.
