# Per Diem

Per diem is the daily allowance for days you are away on business travel or deployed to a site. You submit a per diem request with the destination and dates. The daily rate comes from the per diem rate set on your employee profile.

**Who does what**

| Role | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Employee (everyone) | Submit your own per diem request and track it in **Approvals** and in the **Per Diem** list. |
| Team Lead, Department Head, Discipline Lead | Approve per diem requests that reach you in **Approvals**. |
| HR (HR Manager, HR Assistant, HR Specialist) | Set each employee's per diem rate, see the company-wide list at **/hr/per-diem**, and approve or process requests. |

**Where to find it**
- Submit: **New Request** → **Per Diem** (or open `/approvals/new/per-diem`).
- Track: sidebar → **Pay & Travel** → **Per Diem**. You see your own requests; HR sees everyone's.

---

## Before you start

- You need a per diem rate on your employee profile. If the form says *"No per diem rate assigned"*, contact HR to set it up. You cannot submit until it is set.

---

## How to submit a per diem request (Employee)

1. Go to **New Request** → **Per Diem**.
2. **Destination:** choose the **Destination Country**, then the **Destination City**. The destination category is set from the country automatically. You can change it if needed.
3. **Trip details:**
   - Choose the **Trip type**: *Travel* or *Site deployment*.
   - Optionally link the request to an **approved site assignment** (this fills the city and travel dates), or to an approved **flight** or **business trip** (this fills the destination, dates and category). Leave both empty for a standalone per diem.
   - Enter the **Start Date** and **End Date**, or the **Number of days** if the form asks for it. The number of days cannot exceed the maximum calendar days shown.
   - Enter the **Departure time** and **Return time** if the form asks for them.
4. **Rate:** the **Total Days**, **Daily Rate** and **Total Amount** update as you fill the form.
   - To ask for a higher rate, switch on **Request a different rate?**, enter the **Requested daily rate**, and give a **Reason for rate increase**. The requested rate must be higher than the configured rate.
5. Add **Notes** for the approver if needed.
6. Click **Review & Submit**, check the summary, and click **Submit Request**.

> **What happens next:** The request enters the approval chain. Track it in **Approvals**. If an approver returns it, edit it there and resubmit.

---

## How to track your per diem requests

1. Go to **Pay & Travel** → **Per Diem**.
2. Open a row to see the dates, destination, rate, total amount and approval status.

---

## How HR works with per diem

*(HR Manager, HR Assistant, HR Specialist)*

1. Set each employee's per diem rate under **Pay & Travel** → **Employee Per Diem Rates**. Requests cannot be submitted without a rate.
2. Use **Pay & Travel** → **Per Diem** to see all requests, filter them, and open each one.
3. Review the rate-increase reason on requests that ask for a higher rate before you approve them.

---

## Tips

- Link the request to the approved site assignment or trip. This keeps the dates and destination consistent with the record.
- If the total looks wrong, check the **Total Days** and **Daily Rate** in the summary before you submit.
