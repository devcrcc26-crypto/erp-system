# Per Diem

Per diem is a daily allowance for days you travel or work at a site. You request an **advance** before you go. After the trip, you file a **settlement** that compares the advance you were paid with the days you actually travelled.

**Find your part of this guide**

| You are | Read these sections |
| --- | --- |
| Employee | Requesting an advance, filing a settlement, following your requests, cancelling, and what to do if a request is returned |
| Anyone who approves per diem | How to approve a per diem request |
| HR (HR Manager, HR Assistant, HR Specialist) | Rates, disbursements, settling and recording balances |

---

## Before you start

*(Employees)*

You need a per diem rate on your employee record. If the form shows **No per diem rate assigned**, contact HR. You cannot submit until your rate is set.

---

## How to request a per diem advance

*(Employees)*

1. In the left menu, go to **New Request**.
2. Click **Per Diem Request**. The form has three steps: **Destination Category**, **Request Details** and **Review & Submit**.


## Step 1 — Destination Category

*(Employees)*

1. **Trip type** (required): choose **Travel** or **Site deployment**.
2. **Destination Country** (required), then **Destination City** (required).
3. **Destination Category** (required): it is set from the country automatically (*"Auto-set from country selection. You can change this."*). Change it if needed.
4. Click **Next**.

## Step 2 — Request Details

*(Employees)*

1. Link the request if it belongs to an approved trip:
   - For **Site deployment**: **Site assignment (optional)**. Choosing one fills the city and the dates.
   - For **Travel**: **Linked Travel (Optional)**. Choose an approved flight or business trip to fill the destination, dates and category. Leave it on *"No linked trip or flight (standalone per diem)"* if there is none.
2. **Start Date** and **End Date** (required).
3. **Departure time** and **Return time** (required).
4. **Number of days** (required): it cannot be more than the calendar days between the dates.
5. Check **Total Days**, **Daily Rate** and **Total Amount**.
6. To ask for a higher rate, switch on **Request a different rate?**. Enter the **Requested daily rate** (it must be higher than your configured rate) and the **Reason for rate increase** (required).
7. **Notes** (optional): anything the approver should know.
8. Click **Next**.


## Step 3 — Review & Submit

*(Employees)*

1. Check the destination, dates, days and total.
2. Click **Back** to change anything.
3. Click **Submit Request**.

> **After you submit:** You see *"Per diem request submitted"*. Your request has been sent for approval. You will receive a notification when it is approved or returned.

---

## How to settle an advance after your trip

*(Employees — you need at least one advance that has been approved and paid)*

1. In the left menu, go to **New Request**.
2. Click **Per Diem Settlement**. The **New Per Diem Settlement** form has four steps: **Trip**, **Vehicle**, **Advances taken** and **Review & Submit**.

## Step 1 — Trip

1. **Specific Destination** (required): the city and country.
2. **Departure date**, **Departure time**, **Return date** and **Return time** (required): when you actually left and came back.
3. **Number of days** (required): the per diem days you actually travelled.
4. Click **Next**.

## Step 2 — Vehicle

1. Optional. If you used a vehicle, switch on **Vehicle used** and enter **Km out** and **Km in**. **Km in** must be the same as or more than **Km out**.
2. Click **Next**.

## Step 3 — Advances taken

1. Tick the paid advances you are settling. They must have the same destination category. Advances marked **Awaiting payment** cannot be settled yet.
2. Click **Next**.

## Review & Submit

1. Check the **Calculation**: **Advances taken**, less the per diem you earned, and the balance. It shows **Balance in favor of Employee** or **Balance in favor of Company**.
2. Click **Back** to change anything, or **Submit Request** to send it.


> **After you submit:** You see *"Per diem settlement submitted"*. Your settlement has been sent for approval. You will receive a notification when it is approved or returned. If the balance is in favor of the company, return the cash as you are told.

## How to follow your per diem requests

*(Employees)*

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** and click the **My Requests** tab.
2. Click your per diem request to see its details, what has been paid, and its **Approval Chain**.

If people report to you, you also have **Pay & Travel** → **Per Diem** in the left menu. The **Advances** tab lists your requests and your team's with **Request #**, **Destination**, **Start Date**, **Days**, **Total**, **Paid** (**Disbursed**, **Unpaid** or **Settled**) and **Status**. The **Settlements** tab lists settlements.

**What the statuses mean**

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | Saved but not sent. |
| Submitted | Sent for approval. |
| In Review | Being approved. |
| Returned | Sent back to you to change. |
| Approved | Approved. Waiting to be paid. |
| Active | Paid. Your trip is under way or finished. |
| Rejected | Not approved. |
| Cancelled | Cancelled. |

## How to cancel a per diem request

*(Employees — while the advance is **Draft** or **Submitted**)*

1. Open the request.
2. Click **Cancel**.
3. Confirm **Cancel per diem request?**. This cannot be undone.

A settlement can be cancelled while it is **Draft**, **Submitted** or **Returned**. Its advances are released and can be settled again.

---

## What to do if your request is returned

*(Employees)*

If your request is returned, you receive the notification **Approval returned**. Open the request to see the reason.

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** → **My Requests** and open the request with status **Returned**. The banner says **This request was returned** and shows the approver's note.
2. Read the note.
3. To change the request, click **Edit** at the top of the request (or **Edit** next to it in **My Requests**). The form opens with your details filled in, and the approver's note is shown at the top. Make your changes and click **Save**. You see *"Changes saved."* and go back to the request, which is still **Returned**. The daily rate and total are worked out again from your current per diem rate.
4. When you are ready, click **Resubmit** at the top of the request (if nothing needs to change, you can skip editing). In **Resubmit request**, write a **Note to approver (optional)**, click **Attach files** if needed, and click **Resubmit**.

After you resubmit, the request goes back through the approval chain from the beginning.

> **After you resubmit:** You see *"Request resubmitted"*.

## How to approve a per diem request

*(Anyone who approves per diem)*

When a per diem request reaches you, you receive the notification **Approval required**.

1. In the left menu, go to **Approvals** → **Pending My Action** and open the request.
2. Check the destination, dates, days and total.
3. Click **Approve**. The window shows a **Rate Summary**: the **Configured Rate**, and the **Requested Rate** and **Override Reason** if the employee asked for more.
4. **Approved Daily Rate** (required): it is filled in with the requested rate, or the configured rate. Change it if you approve a different rate.
5. Add a **Note (optional)** and click **Approve**.

To return or reject the request instead, see the **Approvals Inbox** topic.

## How to set an employee's per diem rates

*(HR)*

1. In the left menu, go to **Pay & Travel** → **Employee Per Diem Rates**.
2. The counters show **Employees**, **Configured** and **Not configured**. Search by name, email or department.
3. Click **Add rates** for a new employee, or **Update** on an existing row.
4. For each destination category, enter the **Daily Rate** and set it **Active** or **Inactive**. Inactive categories are hidden from the employee's request form.
5. Click **Save**.

> **Note:** *"This rate looks low for ETB - please verify."* warns you about a rate that may have been entered in the wrong currency.

## How to see all per diem requests

*(HR)*

1. In the left menu, go to **Pay & Travel** → **Per Diem**. **Per Diem Requests** opens.
2. The counters show **Total**, **Draft**, **Pending**, **Approved** and **Rejected**.
3. Search by request number or destination, or filter by **Employee**. Use the **Advances** and **Settlements** tabs.
4. Click a request to open it.

## How to record a disbursement

*(HR — when the request is **Approved** or **Active**)*

1. Open the request.
2. Click **Add Disbursement**. The **Record Disbursement** window opens.
3. Enter the **PV number**, the **PV date**, and the days it **Covers days from** and **Covers days to**. The window shows the amount for those days.
4. **Receipts (optional)**: attach files.
5. Click **Save**.

> **After you save:** You see *"Disbursement recorded"*. The request becomes **Active** and shows **Disbursed**.

## How to settle a request and record the balance

*(HR — when the request is **Active**)*

1. Open the request and click **Settle**. The **Settle Per Diem Request** window opens.
2. Enter the **Actual return date**. To use a different number of days, fill in **Override actual days**. Leave it blank to calculate from the date.
3. Click **Save**. The **Settlement** section shows the **Earned total**, **Total disbursed** and the **Balance**, owed to the employee or to the company.
4. When the balance is paid, click **Record payment to employee** (or **Record cash returned**). Enter the **Payment reference** or **Receipt number** and the date, and click **Save**.

For an employee's **Per Diem Settlement**, open it under the **Settlements** tab. If the balance is in favor of the company, click **Record cash returned**, enter the **Receipt number** and **Date cash returned**, and click **Save**.
