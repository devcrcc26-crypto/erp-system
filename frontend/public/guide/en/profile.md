# My Profile & Notifications

**My Profile** is your own employee record. You can see your details and your leave, overtime, attendance, work schedule, documents, contract, payslips, assets, per diem and evaluations. You can change your regional preferences, email address and password yourself. For anything else, ask HR.

---

## How to open your profile

*(Everyone)*

1. In the top left, click **My Profile**. It is at the bottom and shows your name. 
2. Click a tab to see that part of your record.


| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Account** | Your name, employee details and account settings |
| **Leave** | Your leave balances and history, and your site assignments |
| **Overtime** | Your overtime for the year. Click **View all** for every sheet |
| **Attendance** | Your monthly attendance and any days that need an explanation |
| **Work Schedule** | Your working hours and the public holidays coming up |
| **Documents** | Documents HR has filed for you |
| **Contract** | Your employment contract |
| **Assets** | Company equipment issued to you |
| **Per Diem** | Your per diem rates and requests |
| **Evaluations** | Your performance evaluations |

## How to see your payslips

*(Employees)*

1. Go to **My Profile** → **Payroll**. You see only your own payslips.
2. Each row shows the **Period**, your **Net pay** and the date it was **Released**. A payslip marked **Not released** is still being prepared by HR.
3. Click **View breakdown** to see your earnings, overtime, deductions and net pay.
4. Click **Download PDF** to save or print the payslip.

> **Note:** If you see *"No payslips yet. They appear here once HR releases them."*, HR has not released a payslip for you yet. Ask HR if you think one is missing.

## How to change your language, time zone or date format

*(Everyone)*

1. Go to **My Profile** → **Account**.
2. Under **Regional preferences**, choose the **Language**, **Timezone** and **Date format**. Leave one blank to use your company's default.
3. Click **Save preferences**.

> **After you save:** You see *"Preferences saved."*

> **Note:** You can also switch between English and Chinese at any time with the language control at the top of the screen.

---

## How to change your email address

*(Everyone)*

1. Go to **My Profile** → **Account**.
2. Under **Email address**, click **Change email address**.
3. Enter the **New email address** and click **Send confirmation**.
4. Open the email at your new address and click the link.

> **After you send it:** Your profile shows *"Pending confirmation: [new email]"* until you click the link. Your old address keeps working until then.

If your profile shows **Email not verified**, click **Resend activation email** and use the link in that email.

---

## How to change your password

*(Everyone)*

1. Go to **My Profile** → **Account**.
2. Under **Password**, enter your **Current password**.
3. Enter the **New password** (at least 8 characters) and type it again in **Confirm new password**.
4. Click **Change password**.

> **After you change it:** You see *"Password changed. Other sessions have been signed out."* Sign in again on your other devices with the new password.

---

## How to use notifications

*(Everyone)*

You get a notification when something needs you, for example when a request is approved or returned, an approval is waiting, or an announcement is published.

1. Click the bell at the top of the screen. The number shows how many are unread.
2. Click **Unread** to see only new ones, or **All**.
3. Click a notification to open the request or page it is about. Click **Mark read** on one, or **Mark all read** for all of them.
4. Click **View all notifications** to open the full list.
