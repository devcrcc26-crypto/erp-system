# My Profile & Notifications

**My Profile** is your own employee record. It shows your details and your leave, overtime, attendance, work schedule, documents, contract, payroll, assets, per diem and evaluations. You can change only your preferences, email address and password.

**Who does what**

| Role | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Employee (everyone) | View your own profile and tabs. Change your language, time zone, date format, email and password. |
| HR | Manages employee records. Employees edit only what is listed above. |

**Where to find it**
- Sidebar → **My Profile** (at the bottom of the sidebar, next to your name).

---

## The profile tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Account | Your name, employee details, and your account settings. |
| Leave | Your leave balances and leave history. Your site assignments are here too. |
| Overtime | Your overtime for the year, by category, and your sheet history. |
| Attendance | Your monthly attendance and flagged days. Submit explanations here. |
| Work Schedule | Your working schedule and the public holidays that apply to you in the next 60 days. |
| Documents | Documents HR has filed for you. |
| Contract | Your employment contract details. |
| Payroll | Your payslips. You can see only your own. |
| Assets | Company assets assigned to you. |
| Per Diem | Your per diem rate and requests. |
| Evaluations | Your performance evaluations. |

---

## How to change your preferences

1. Go to **My Profile** → **Account**.
2. Change the **Language**, **Time zone** or **Date format** and save.

---

## How to change your email or password

1. Go to **My Profile** → **Account**.
2. **Change email:** enter the new email address and save.
3. **Change password:** enter your current password, then the new password twice, and save.

---

## Notifications

Use the bell icon in the top bar to see notifications, such as approval updates and announcements. Click a notification to open the related request or page.
