# Company Assets

The company asset register lists the equipment the company owns, such as laptops, tools and machines. It records each item's serial number, status, who has it, and its value over time. Employees can see the assets issued to them.

**Find your part of this guide**

| You are | Read these sections |
| --- | --- |
| Employee | Seeing your assets, and reporting a problem with one |
| Fleet and HR staff | Adding assets, issuing and taking back assets, changing status, and exporting or importing |

---

## How to see the assets issued to you

*(Employees)*

You cannot add, issue or change company assets; Fleet and HR staff manage them. You can see the ones issued to you.

1. In the left menu, go to **My Profile**.
2. Click the **Assets** tab. **Assigned Company Assets** lists each asset's **Photo**, **Asset Name**, **Serial Number**, **Type**, **Status** and **Assigned At** date.


> **Note:** If you see *"No company assets are currently assigned to you."* but you have company equipment, ask HR to record it.

---

## How to report a problem with an asset

*(Employees)*

1. In the left menu, go to **New Request**.
2. Click **Inspection and Repair**.
3. Under **Asset Source**, choose **Use assigned company asset** and pick the asset.
4. Fill in the **Request Details** and submit. See the **Vehicles** topic for each step.

## How to add a company asset

*(Fleet and HR staff)*

1. In the left menu, go to **Operations** → **Fleet & Assets** → **Company Assets**.
2. Click **New Asset**. The form has four steps: **Asset Details**, **Fixed Asset Ledger**, **Photo** and **Review & Submit**.


## Step 1 — Asset Details

*(Fleet and HR staff)*

1. **Serial Number** (required): exactly as it appears on the item. It must be unique.
2. **Type** (required), **Brand** (required), **Model** (required) and **Year** (required).
3. **Color** and **Description** (optional).
4. Click **Next**.

## Step 2 — Fixed Asset Ledger

*(Fleet and HR staff — optional, for tracking value)*

1. **Asset Code**, **Specifications** and **Asset Category**.
2. **Start of Use Date** and **Useful Life (Months)**.
3. **Original Value**.
4. **Managing Department** and **Storage Location**.
5. Click **Next**.

> **Note:** With a **Start of Use Date**, **Useful Life (Months)** and **Original Value**, the asset page works out the **Depreciation Summary**: **Monthly Depreciation**, **Months Depreciated**, **Accumulated Depreciation** and **Net Value**.

## Step 3 — Photo

*(Fleet and HR staff)*

1. Click **Upload Photo** to add a clear JPG, PNG or WEBP photo. This step is optional.
2. Click **Next**.

## Step 4 — Review & Submit

*(Fleet and HR staff)*

1. Check the details.
2. Click **Create Asset**.

> **After you save:** You see *"Asset created successfully"*. The new asset is **Available**.

## How to issue an asset to an employee

*(Fleet and HR staff)*

1. Open the asset from **Company Assets**.
2. Under **Current Assignment**, click **Issue Asset**. The asset must be **Available**. Otherwise you see *"Change the asset status to Available before issuing it."*
3. In **Issue asset**, choose the **Employee** and add **Notes** if needed.
4. Click **Issue Asset**.


> **After you issue it:** You see *"Asset issued successfully"*. The status becomes **In Use**, and the asset appears on the employee's **Assets** tab.

---

## How to take an asset back

*(Fleet and HR staff)*

1. Open the asset.
2. Click **Return Asset**.
3. In **Return asset**, enter where it was **Returned To** (for example *"Main Store"*) and any **Notes**.
4. Click **Return Asset**. You see *"Asset returned successfully"*, and the return is now waiting to be acknowledged.
5. When you have the item in hand, click **Acknowledge Return**. Check **Returned To** and the **Notes**, then click **Acknowledge Return** again.

> **After you acknowledge:** You see *"Asset return acknowledged"*. The asset is **Available** again, and the handover is kept under **Assignment History**.

---

## How to change, edit or delete an asset

*(Fleet and HR staff)*

- **Change status:** click **Change Status** and choose the **New status**: **Available**, **Repairing** or **Disposed**. **In Use** is set automatically when you issue the asset.
- **Edit:** click **Edit Asset**, change the details, and click **Save**.
- **Delete:** click **Delete Asset** and confirm **Delete asset?**. This cannot be undone.

---

## How to find, export or import assets

*(Fleet and HR staff)*

1. On **Company Assets**, the counters show **Total**, **Available**, **In use** and **Repairing**.
2. Search by serial number, brand, model or the person it is assigned to, or filter by **Type**.
3. Click **Export assets** to download the register.
4. Click **Import from Excel** to add many assets at once. Imported assets are **Available**, and serial numbers must be unique.
