# Payroll (HR)

Payroll runs once a month. You create the run, the ERP calculates each employee's pay from their contract, attendance, overtime and leave, and you check, submit and approve it. After the salaries are paid, you generate the payslips and release them to employees.

**Find your part of this guide**

| You are | You can |
| --- | --- |
| HR Assistant, HR Manager | Create, check, recalculate, submit, mark as paid and cancel runs; add top-ups and freelancers; generate and release payslips; export |
| HR Manager | Everything above, plus **Approve (HR)** and company payroll settings |
| General Manager | **GM approve**, export, and view company settings |
| Managing Director | Export and view company settings |

**Where to find it**
- In the left menu, go to **Pay & Travel** → **Payroll**. The **Payroll runs** list opens.

> **Important:** Salary figures are confidential. Only people with payroll access can open this page.

---

## The steps of a run

Every run moves through these steps, shown at the top of the run:

| Step | What happens | Who acts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draft | The run is created and calculated. You check and adjust it. | HR |
| Reviewed | HR has checked it. | HR |
| Submitted | Sent to the HR Manager. | HR |
| HR approved | The HR Manager approved it. | HR Manager |
| GM approved | The General Manager approved it. | General Manager |
| Paid | Salaries have been paid. Payslips can be released. | HR |

---

## How to create a payroll run

*(HR Assistant, HR Manager)*

1. In the left menu, go to **Pay & Travel** → **Payroll**.
2. Click **New run**. The **New payroll run** window opens.
3. **Pay period** (required): choose the month.
4. **Notes** (optional).
5. Click **Create & generate**.


> **After you create it:** You see *"Payroll run created."* The run is a **Draft** with one entry per employee on the payroll roster. Only one run can exist per month. If you try another, you see *"Payroll run for [month] already exists"*.

---

## How to check a run

*(HR Assistant, HR Manager)*

1. Open the run from the **Payroll runs** list. The list shows **Run #**, **Period**, **Status**, **Employees**, **Total gross**, **Total net**, **Flagged** and **Created**.
2. If you see *"[N] flagged entries need review."*, click **Show flagged only**.
3. Filter **Entries** by **Department** or **Discipline**, or search.
4. Click **Details** on an entry to see its breakdown, including **Paid days** (on a 30-day month basis).
5. Read each entry's **Flag** and **Flag note**.


**Flags you may see**

| Flag | Meaning | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Missing attendance | No attendance for the month. | Import and approve the month's attendance, then click **Recalculate**. |
| Missing contract | No contract covers the month. | Add or renew the contract, then click **Recalculate**. |
| Overtime not approved | The month's overtime is not approved yet. | Have the overtime approved, then click **Recalculate**. |
| Zero salary | The salary is zero. | Check the contract. |
| Manual override | A figure was changed by hand. | Check it is correct. |
| Leaver - final month | The employee leaves this month. | Check the final pay. |
| Login disabled, still employed | The login is off but the employee is still employed. | Check the employee's status. |
| Inactive in month | The employee was inactive for part of the month. | Check the paid days. |
| Contract ended - not renewed | The contract ended and was not renewed. | Renew the contract or confirm the employee left. |

### Employment review

If you see *"[N] employee(s) need an employment review before you continue"*, check each listed employee, then tick **I've reviewed these [N] employees**. You cannot continue until you do.

## How to add a bonus or net top-up

*(HR Assistant, HR Manager — while the run is **Draft** or **Reviewed**)*

1. On the entry, click **Edit bonus / net top-up**.
2. Enter the **Bonus top-up (ETB)** or the **Net top-up (ETB)**. The amount must be 0 or more.
3. **Reason** (required if the amount is not 0).
4. Check the preview, for example *"Net pay will increase by … ETB"*.
5. Save.

> **After you save:** You see *"Top-up saved."* Top-ups and notes are kept when the run is recalculated.

---

## How to add or remove a freelancer

*(HR Assistant, HR Manager)*

Freelancers are never added automatically.

1. Click **Add freelancer**. The **Add freelancers to this payroll** window opens.
2. **Freelancers**: search and choose who is paid this month. Only active freelancers on the payroll roster who are not already in the run appear.
3. Click **Add to payroll**.

To remove one, click **Remove from payroll** on their entry and confirm. Regular employees cannot be removed this way.

> **Note:** If the run is already in approval, adding or removing a freelancer sends it back to **Draft**. It must be reviewed and approved again.

---

## How to recalculate a run

*(HR Assistant, HR Manager)*

Recalculate after you change contracts, attendance, overtime or leave for the month, or when you see *"Attendance changed after this run was generated - regenerate"*.

1. Click **Recalculate**.
2. Confirm **Recalculate payroll?**.

> **After you recalculate:** You see *"Entries recalculated."* Top-ups, notes and added freelancers are kept. If the run had been submitted or approved, it goes back to **Draft** and must be reviewed and approved again.

---

## How to mark a run as reviewed and submit it

*(HR Assistant, HR Manager)*

1. When the **Draft** is correct, click **Mark reviewed** and confirm **Mark as reviewed?**.
2. Click **Submit for approval** and confirm **Submit for approval?**. If any entries are flagged, tick **I've reviewed the flagged entries.** first.

> **After you submit:** You see *"Run submitted for approval."* The run waits for the HR Manager.

---

## How to approve a payroll run as HR

*(HR Manager)*

1. Open a run with the step **Submitted**.
2. Check the totals and the flagged entries.
3. Click **Approve (HR)** and confirm **Approve payroll run?**.

> **After you approve:** You see *"Run approved by HR."* The run waits for the General Manager.

---

## How to approve a payroll run as General Manager

*(General Manager)*

1. In the left menu, go to **Pay & Travel** → **Payroll** and open the run with the step **HR approved**.
2. Check the totals and the entries.
3. Click **GM approve** and confirm **GM approve payroll run?**.


> **After you approve:** You see *"Run approved by GM."* HR can now pay the salaries and release the payslips.

If something needs to change, do not approve. Tell HR what to fix. HR changes and recalculates the run, which sends it back to **Draft** to be reviewed and approved again.

---

## What to do if the General Manager asks for changes

*(HR Assistant, HR Manager)*

1. Make the change: adjust the contract, attendance, overtime or top-up as needed.
2. Open the run and click **Recalculate**. The run goes back to **Draft**.
3. Check the entries, then click **Mark reviewed** and **Submit for approval** again.
4. The HR Manager approves it again, then the General Manager.

---

## How to mark a run as paid

*(HR Assistant, HR Manager — after **GM approved**)*

1. Open the run with the step **GM approved**.
2. Click **Mark as paid** and confirm **Mark as paid?**.

> **After you confirm:** You see *"Run marked as paid."*

---

## How to generate and release payslips

*(HR Assistant, HR Manager — when the run is **GM approved** or **Paid**)*

1. Open the run and go to **Payslips**.
2. Click **Generate payslips**. You see how many were created, updated and skipped.
3. Click **Open payslip** on an employee to check the PDF.
4. Click **Release to employees** and confirm **Release payslips to employees?**.
5. To download them all, click **Download all (ZIP)**.


> **After you release:** You see *"Released. [N] employees notified."* Each employee sees their payslip under **My Profile** → **Payroll**.

---

## How to export payroll

*(HR Assistant, HR Manager, General Manager, Managing Director)*

1. Open the run and click **Export**.
2. Choose **Salary sheet (Excel)** or **Bank letter (Word)**.
3. The bank letter is only available when the run is **GM approved** or **Paid**. If some employees have no bank account, you see **Employees missing bank accounts**. Their account cell is left blank for you to fill in. Click **Download letter**.

---

## How to cancel a run

*(HR Assistant, HR Manager — while the run is **Draft** or **Reviewed**)*

1. Open the run.
2. Click **Cancel run** and confirm **Cancel payroll run?**.

> **After you cancel:** You see *"Run cancelled."* You can create a new run for the same month.

---

## How to set up company payroll details

*(HR Manager edits; General Manager and Managing Director can view)*

1. In **Pay & Travel** → **Payroll**, click **Company settings**.
2. Fill in **Tax declaration** (**Organisation name (tax forms)**, **Company TIN**, **Tax account no.**, **Tax collector office**), **Address & contact** (**Region**, **Zone**, **Woreda**, **Kebele**, **House no.**, **Phone**, **Fax**) and **Salary bank letter** (**Salary bank name**, **Bank branch**, **Company account number**, **Bank letter reference prefix**).
3. Click **Save settings**.

These details fill the tax declaration sheet and the bank transfer letter.

---

## Things HR should know

*(HR)*

- **Locked overtime.** Once a run is finalized, that month's overtime cannot be changed.
- **Attendance changed after approval.** If you see *"Attendance changed after this run was generated - review required"*, check the attendance change, then recalculate if needed.
- **Policy changed after approval.** If you see *"An HR policy changed for this month after the run was approved - review required"*, the run is not recalculated automatically. Decide whether to recalculate.
